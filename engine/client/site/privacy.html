Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 October 2026

Turnip Works (turnipworks.net and the game served there) is run by Implausible Enterprises LLC, a Delaware limited liability company operating from California ("we", "us", "our"). This policy says what we collect, why, who else sees it, and what you can do about it. If we ever add something it doesn't cover, we'll update it first, before the change goes live.

The short version. There are no accounts. We keep what you put on your tables so the game works. You can choose to email yourself your table link; if you do, we may also use your address, in scrambled (hashed) form, to show Turnip Works ads on Meta, Google and Reddit, unless you opt out or are in Europe, the UK or Switzerland. We count how many tables actually get played, on our own server, with no trackers in the game. We run no ad trackers or analytics scripts today. No one pays us for your information.

What we collect today

What's on your tables. Everything you and the people at your table put into the game: rooms, buildings, pawns, notes, the names you give yourselves, chat and dice rolls. It's stored on our server so that everyone at the table sees the same thing, and it's backed up. We use it only to run, back up, fix and improve the game.

Seat links. Each seat at a table has a link with a key in it. Our server keeps the keys so it can check them. Your browser keeps your links, your list of games and your settings in its local storage, on your device. We don't set cookies of our own.

Feedback you send. If you use Settings ▸ Feedback, we get what you write, a screenshot of your view of the table, and technical details that help us fix bugs: your browser and operating system, screen size, graphics card, language, game version, and which table and seat it came from.

Corrections to the VTT comparison index. If you send one from the form on the VTT comparison index, we get what you write, which product and column it's about, whether you ticked "I make this product", and any contact you choose to type in. We use it to correct the table and, if you left a contact, to answer you. It isn't posted anywhere. To limit abuse, our game server holds your IP address in memory for about an hour after you send a correction; it isn't stored with it.

Logs. Like any website, the services in front of Turnip Works see your IP address, your browser's user agent, the address asked for and the time. Cloudflare, which delivers the site, handles this under its own policy. Our web server keeps its logs for about 14 days, except for the game's own API requests, which it doesn't log because their addresses carry seat keys. Our game server also keeps IP addresses in memory for up to an hour to limit how fast new tables can be made, which stops abuse. We use logs only to keep the service running and safe.

"Email yourself your link." In the game you can type in your email address to get your link sent to you (the GM's link, or your own seat's), so you don't lose it. It's always optional, and nothing in the game depends on it. If you do, we keep your address with: which table and seat it's for, whether you're the GM or a player, whether you ticked the news box, the country our network provider (Cloudflare) reports for your connection at that moment, and whether your browser sent a Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal. We use it like this:

  • To send you your link. That email contains your link and the facts about who sent it, and nothing promotional. We send it once each time you ask.
  • For occasional news about Turnip Works, only if you tick the box for it. The box starts unticked. You can stop news at any time with the link in our emails.
  • To show you Turnip Works ads, and to find people like you. We may upload a hashed copy of your email address to Meta (Facebook and Instagram), Google and Reddit. Hashing turns the address into a scrambled code before it leaves us, but it's not anonymous: each platform hashes its own users' addresses the same way to find matches. A platform uses a match to show Turnip Works ads to that person, to leave them out of ads, or to find other people with similar interests to show our ads to. The platforms say they discard codes that don't match one of their users. We use the lists only to advertise Turnip Works, and the platforms' advertiser terms limit what they may do with them. Their own privacy policies cover what they do as advertisers: Meta, Google, Reddit.
  • Not in Europe, the UK or Switzerland. If the country Cloudflare reports when you give us your address is in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, or the country can't be told (for example over Tor), we never use your address for ads, even if you later connect from somewhere else. We still send you your link, and news if you ask for it.
  • Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track. If your browser sends either signal when you give us your address, we treat that as an opt-out from ads from the start.
  • Opting out and deleting. Every email we send has a link to a page where, with one press, you can stop us ever using your address for ads, stop news, or delete your address altogether. You can also ask us (see "Contact"). When you opt out of ads or delete your address, we keep only a hashed copy, so the address never goes back into our ad lists.

On a phone: "email yourself the link." If you open turnipworks.net on a phone, the page offers to email you a link to open later on your computer. It works like "Email yourself your link" above, with the same address book, uses, limits and choices, except that it's for no table or seat: the link we send carries the campaign tags and any ad click code of the link you arrived by (our server keeps a click code only where ads are allowed, as above), and a code that lets our server note, with no address, that the page was shown, that the link was sent and that it was later opened, so we can count a phone visit that became a table on a computer.

To stop the link email being used to flood someone's inbox, we limit how many link emails go to one address, from one seat and from one network; the network part is held in memory for up to a day and not written down.

Counting tables that get played. To know whether Turnip Works is any use (and whether our ads are), our game server notes, for each new table: when it was started, how many invites were made, when someone other than the GM first joined, whether the GM stayed on the table for at least three minutes and did something there (placed, moved or built something, rolled a die or sent an invite), the country Cloudflare reports for the GM's connection, and the campaign tags on the link the GM arrived by, if it had any (for example "utm_source=reddit", and the address of the site that linked to us). If the GM's country may be used for ads (see above), it also keeps the ad click code an ad link carries, so a future ad report can say "this ad led to a table being played". It keeps no IP address, no name and no email for this. This happens on our own server; there are no trackers in the game.

A code library from jsDelivr. The game loads its 3D library (three.js) from jsDelivr, a public code delivery network, which sees your IP address and browser like any web server does.

AI assistants. If you give a seat link to a chat AI, that AI's service reads whatever the seat can see, including the table and its chat, under that service's own privacy policy. That's your choice, and it's between you and that service.

What we don't do today. We don't ask for your name, and we ask for your email only when you choose to email yourself your link. We don't run ad pixels, analytics or tracking scripts, and no one else collects information about your activity across other websites through Turnip Works.

If we add ad pixels

We don't put ad pixels or tracking scripts on any of our pages, and none in the game. If we ever decide to add them to our marketing pages, we'll update this policy first, saying exactly what they collect and how to switch them off.

Who else sees your information

  • The people at your table see what's on it, except what the GM hides from players.
  • Companies that run parts of the service for us, such as Cloudflare (which delivers the site) and Google (Google Workspace, which delivers our emails, so it sees the address and the email). They handle data to provide their service to us.
  • jsDelivr, which delivers the game's 3D code library and so sees your IP address and browser.
  • Meta, Google and Reddit, as advertisers' platforms: hashed email addresses as described in "Email yourself your link".
  • The AI services you choose to give a seat link to.
  • If the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety, or to protect Turnip Works and its users from fraud or abuse.
  • Whoever takes over Turnip Works, if it's ever transferred to another company, which would have to keep this policy's promises for information collected under it.

We don't sell your personal information for money. No one pays us for it. Some privacy laws may count the hashed emails described above as "sharing" or even a "sale" for advertising, even though no money changes hands. That is the only such use, and you can opt out of them as described above.

How long we keep it

  • Tables stay on our server until they're deleted. There's no delete button in the game yet: the GM asks us. A deleted table is first moved aside, in case the deletion was a mistake, and stays there until we clear it out; we don't do that on a fixed schedule yet. If you ask us to delete a table for good, we remove it from both places. Inactive tables may be deleted some day (see the Terms of Use).
  • Backups: we keep the most recent 30 backup copies of the server's tables and delete older ones, so something deleted stays in the backups until the copies made before its deletion have all been replaced. That isn't a fixed number of days.
  • Server logs last about 14 days.
  • Feedback and corrections are kept as long as they're useful for fixing things.
  • Email addresses: until you delete yours (the link in any of our emails) or ask us to. A deleted address stays in our backup copies until the copies made before its deletion have all been replaced, as for tables.
  • The table counts (when a table was started, invited, joined and played): as long as they're useful for counting; they hold no name, email or IP address.

Your choices

  • See, correct or delete your information. Ask us (see "Contact"). Since there are no accounts, tell us which table and seat, or which email address, so we can find it. We may ask you to show that the seat or the address is yours, for example by sending the request from that seat or replying from that address, and we won't hand you other people's information from a shared table.
  • Delete a table. The GM can ask us to delete it (send the request from the GM's seat).
  • Clear your browser. Clearing this site's data removes the links and settings your browser kept. Save your links first.
  • Stop news, opt out of ads, or delete your address: the link in any of our emails, or ask us.
  • Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control. We don't track you across websites. If your browser sends either signal when you give us your email, we never use that address for ads.

Children

Turnip Works is for people 13 and older, and isn't meant for children. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that an email address belongs to a child under 13, we'll send them the link they asked for and then delete the address, and it will never be used for news or ads. If we learn that a child under 13 is using a table, we'll delete any personal information we know came from them and may remove their seat. If we learn an address belongs to anyone under 18, we never use it for ads. If you're a parent and think your child under 13 has given us information, tell us and we'll delete it.

Security, and where your data lives

Our servers are in the United States, and we take reasonable steps to protect what we keep. No system is perfectly secure, though, and on Turnip Works your seat link is the key to your seat: anyone who has it gets in. Keep it private.

Changes to this policy

We may change this policy at any time. A change takes effect when we post it here, with a new "Last updated" date at the top. When a change is a big one, we'll try to say so in the game too. We won't use information we already have in a materially different way than this policy described when we collected it without asking you first. The Terms of Use say more about changes.

Contact

Email hello@turnipworks.net. You can also use Settings ▸ Feedback in the game, which comes privately to us rather than being posted publicly, or GitHub issues, which are public, so don't put personal information there.
