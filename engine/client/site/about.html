About Turnip Works

Last updated 21 October 2026

Turnip Works is a 3D virtual tabletop that runs in a browser tab. You open it, you start a table, you send your players a link. They click it and they're in. Nothing to install, no signup, no account to forget the password for. It's free.

We wanted the speed of a 2D battle map with the feel of a real table full of miniatures. So you drop a building on the table and it furnishes itself in a few seconds, down to the candles. The dice are real 3D dice that tumble across the table. Fog of war knows about darkvision and where the lamps are. You can type "spike trap", "roll initiative" or "send to the dungeon" into the command bar and it does the thing. And if you want an AI to help run your game, any chat assistant that can open a web link can sit at the table as your GM's helper, through a plain web API.

Vibecoded, and proud of it

Turnip Works is vibecoded: built with AI, by a tiny team, fast. AI coding agents write most of the code. People play it, break it, and decide what stays. That's how two of us went from nothing to a 3D tabletop with buildings, lighting, fog and physics dice in about a month, and it's why new things land every week.

Yours to keep

The engine is open source under the GNU AGPL. The assets, every chair, wall, barrel, banner and goblin in the library, are released under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. Use them in your own games, change them, sell things made with them. Just credit Turnip Works.

That's yours to modify, and it can never be taken away. A Creative Commons licence can't be revoked, by us or by whoever owns us later. Some of us remember a game company trying to pull back a licence that a whole hobby had built on. This one stays put.

The one thing we keep is the name. "Turnip Works" and the turnip logo aren't part of either licence, so if you fork it, give your fork its own name.

How the art is made

The furniture, figures and textures were generated with AI image and 3D models, then picked, fixed and arranged by hand. The licensing notes say which models, and how.

Who makes it

Turnip Works is made by Fauna and giblfiz. Fauna is an AI, giblfiz's collaborator, and does most of the building. giblfiz designs it, steers it, plays it, and has opinions about every lantern. It's published by Implausible Enterprises LLC, a small company in California.
