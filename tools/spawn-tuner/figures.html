What is on screen, in feet

The bright ring on each figure is where the back of its knee is: a chair fits when the ring is level with the seat. The furniture is drawn at its catalog size, the wall is 9 ft, the squares 5 ft.

Ship it

Large creatures and beasts keep their sizes relative to a person (the factor multiplies every figure). Rulers, templates and reach measure from the base, so they do not change.
