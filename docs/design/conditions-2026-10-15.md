# Conditions on the table — design agreed 2026-10-15 (NOT built; do it with the orbital/ring rebuild)

Harry: don't mark conditions with icons on the table — "let's look at more fun ways". The icons are for the ring's
Conditions arc only. Overall direction: **loud — "big cartoony, easy to follow."** No "who is doing it to you" tethers:
"players can keep track."

| Condition | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Prone | figure laid on its side (reuse the Roll / tilt machinery) |
| Unconscious | figure on its back + big drifting Z's |
| Dead (not a 5e condition, add it) | toppled, greyed, no Z's |
| Charmed | orbiter: pink hearts round the head |
| Stunned | orbiter: cartoon stars |
| Frightened | orbiter: a little purple ghost or "!!" (pick from stills) |
| Blinded | orbiter: crossed-out eye or a little black storm cloud (pick from stills) |
| Grappled | orbiter: a grabbing hand |
| Restrained | orbiter: chain and padlock |
| Poisoned | orbiter: green skull-and-crossbones or rising green bubbles |
| Paralyzed (add) | orbiter: blue lightning sparks |
| Invisible | SHIMMER for other players (a refraction ripple, no figure); rippling glass for owner and GM, deliberately unlike the GM-hidden ghost. "Gone" is just the existing Hide button. |
| Incapacitated (add) | the base ring goes grey |
| Concentrating (add, not a condition) | a slowly turning rune circle on the base |
| Exhaustion 1–6 (add) | the base rim as a 6-segment drain ring, yellow → orange → red, level 6 = whole ring black + skull. Harry liked cracks too (1 + 3) but chose "just 1, keep it simple". |

Dropped: petrified, deafened ("they don't really come up").

Rules: orbiters share ONE ring over the head, evenly spaced, shrinking a little as more join; past ~4 show three + "+N";
distinct shape AND colour each. Pose priority: unconscious/dead beat prone. Effects are shared sprites / tints (cheap);
the Low preset and "reduce motion" park orbiters still above the head. Visible to everyone (conditions are not secrets),
except Invisible as above.
