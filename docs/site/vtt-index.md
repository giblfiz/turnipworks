# VTT comparison index

A virtual tabletop (VTT) is the software a group uses to play a tabletop roleplaying game together online: a shared map, tokens or miniatures, dice, and usually a way to hide what the players haven't found yet. This page compares [[COUNT]] of them, and a few related tools, by the features people ask about: the established products, and the new and indie ones their makers have posted on r/VTT, the Reddit community for virtual tabletops.

**Who made this table.** We make Turnip Works, one of the VTTs below. This table is our own development checklist, so expect our row to fill up over time; it is scored by the same rules as every other row, by what's on our live site. **Claim yours / correct me:** if you make one of these or we got something wrong, [tell us](#correct). ## How to read it {: #how-to-read-it }

Our row is scored by the same rules as everyone else's, and every row is checked on the same schedule. A feature of ours that is built but only on our development server, not yet on the live site, is mentioned in the note as "dev (not yet live)" and does not count until it is live.

Other good reasons to pick a VTT, such as official licensed adventures, campaign organisation, voice and video, or a big community library, aren't columns yet; they show up in each product's upsides.

Each cell says *yes*, *partial*, *no*, *n/a* (not applicable to that kind of tool) or *?*. A *?* means we don't know: we didn't find it, which is not the same as it being missing. A value marked (?) is one we couldn't confirm from a source. Values describe the product as people can get it today: planned or unreleased work is in the note, not the value. A product that isn't open to the public yet is described from its maker's own account, and its Stage says so. An official extension from the maker counts as built in; a third-party add-on counts as partial. Open a product's name for the details, with a link to where each claim came from. We did not test every product by hand. The established products were checked against their own sites and documentation. The new and indie rows come from their makers' Reddit posts and their sites as read on 24 October 2026; since then we checked only whether each site is still up. Each row shows both dates.
