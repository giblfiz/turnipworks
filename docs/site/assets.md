# Assets

Everything you can put on a Turnip Works table is here: [[THINGS]] props in [[LOOKS]] looks across [[ROOMS]] rooms, and [[FIGURES]] figures. Packs follow the game's Build menu, pack, then room. Each thing has its own link, a download of the file the game itself loads, and a button that opens it in Turnip Works ready to place.

## Using them

The assets are free to use, change and share, in your own games and anywhere else, under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The one condition is credit. Put this line wherever you list your sources:

[[CREDITLINE]]

Each prop's download is a GLB, the 3D format the game loads; floor tiles and banners are WebP pictures. They open in Blender and most 3D tools, and in other virtual tabletops that take GLB models.

**In Turnip Works** you don't need to download anything: every asset is already in the game. As the GM, find it in the Build menu under its pack and room, or type its name in the command bar. "Use it in Turnip Works" on this page does the same in one step: your table opens with that thing ready, and nothing goes down until you click where you want it.
