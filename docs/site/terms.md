## 4. Your table links

There are no accounts. Each person at a table has a seat link, and the link is the key: anyone who has it can use that seat, and anyone with the GM's link can do anything the GM can, including changing or removing anything on the table. Keep your links private, and share a seat link only with the person it's for.

Your browser remembers your links in its local storage. If you clear your browser's data, use a different browser or device, or lose your link some other way, you may lose your way back to the table. Because we don't know who you are, we usually can't tell whether a lost link is yours, so we usually can't give it back. Save your link somewhere safe.
