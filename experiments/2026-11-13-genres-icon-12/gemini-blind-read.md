## Options to Drop

- **Option L — DROP:** The flying saucer is distinctly 1950s retro pulp (directly violating the modern space-opera requirement), its comedic "dragon riding a UFO" concept feels out of place for a core build tray, and the overlapping anatomy becomes completely illegible at 32 px.
- **Option B — DROP:** The abrupt rectangular side-crops look like a sliced banner rather than a self-contained UI icon, the tiny gap between the dragon and ship closes at 32 px, and the composition communicates a two-faction PvP battle rather than an asset category.
- **Option G — DROP:** Three full-body miniatures side-by-side collapse into a muddy horizontal smear at 32 px, and placing figures on round miniature bases universally signals a "Character / Token / Pawn" tool rather than an environment genre filter.
