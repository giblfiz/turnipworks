This sheet shows small round painted UI icons from the ring menu of a browser 3D tabletop game. The top row R1..R4 are
existing icons of one set. Below are ten new icons, lettered A..J, each at 62 px and (under it) at its real 31 px.
FIRST read every letter label on the sheet. Then, for each of A..J: describe in a few words what it shows, and say YES or
NO: does it belong to the same set as R1..R4 (same circular ground, rim, painted brushwork, bevels, lighting, palette;
same artist in the same sitting)? If NO, say exactly why. Then: among A..J there is a family of six that should differ
ONLY in colour; name each one's colour as you see it and say whether any two are too alike to tell apart at 31 px.
End with one line per letter: `X: YES|NO - <short reason>`.
