#### e) Honest Verdict: Cheap Game Effect vs. Painted-Miniature-World Effect
* **Verdict:** It looks like a **cheap game effect** (a classic additive scrolling-noise cylinder/billboard VFX placed over a 3D mesh), **not** a painted-miniature-world (Object Source Lighting / OSL) effect.
* **What makes it look cheap:**
  * **Glowing fog in empty air instead of light on surfaces:** Real miniature OSL is defined by pigment on *solid forms* (bright glazed highlights on sculpted armor edges, weapon tips, base rims, and floorboards). Here, almost all the effect is semi-transparent glowing sheets hanging in empty air around the pawn.
  * **Additive haze flattening the miniature:** Instead of increasing light-to-shadow contrast on the miniature's sculpt, the translucent front pass fogs over the figure (especially L4 and `silver`), flattening the paint job.
  * **Digital particle dots & warm-on-warm wash:** The tiny white pixel specks read as digital particle emitters, and the default gold hue blends into the orange-lit wooden floor rather than carving out contrast.
* **Three most important changes to move toward a painted-miniature look:**
  1. **Put the light onto the miniature and base (true OSL), not in the air in front of it:** Cut the front-facing volumetric opacity by ~70% and drive the intensity into shader-based directional/Fresnel rim lighting on the miniature's raised sculpted edges and a crisp, painted-style radial gradient on the grey plastic base top and bevel.
  2. **Keep the miniature crisp in silhouette against a back-halo:** Mask or hollow out the aura directly in front of the figure's face and torso so the painted mini stays dark/high-contrast in the foreground against a bright halo behind it—the way miniature painters actually stage OSL backdrops.
  3. **Eliminate the straight cylinder walls and anchor the floor contact:** Use a softer radial + vertical falloff so the outer tube edges on L3–L5 never show as straight lines, and add subtle ambient darkening just outside the base pool so the glow reads as physical light on painted wood.
