#### e) Honest verdict: Cheap game effect vs. Painted-miniature-world effect
* **Verdict**: **CHEAP GAME EFFECT.** It reads as a classic MMO/RTS billboard particle + neon unit-ring overlay pasted on top of 3D miniatures.
* **What makes it look cheap**:
  1. **Glowing smoke in empty air**: In a physical miniature world, air is invisible; floating alpha-blended clouds, particle flecks, and a 2D sprite hanging in mid-air cannot exist on a painted mini without physical sculpt.
  2. **Additive white-core neon rings**: The base rings saturate to pure `#FFFFFF` neon tubes, looking like RTS selection circles rather than painted bases or tabletop lighting.
  3. **Flat tinting instead of directional OSL**: Instead of catching the miniature's raised edges/folds while leaving crevices in shadow, the effect puts a flat translucent wash over the figure and a uniform blur over the floor that ignores the wood-plank crevices.
* **Three most important changes to move toward a painted-miniature-world (OSL) look**:
  1. **Move light from "mid-air billboards" onto 3D surfaces (true OSL)**: Cut the opacity of the floating air haze by 70%+ and drive the intensity through actual directional/point lighting on the miniature's geometry (bright coloured rim/zenithal highlights on raised edges, preserved dark shadows in recesses) and normal-mapped light spill on the floorboards and back wall.
  2. **Treat the base like a painted rim or sculpted translucent resin**: Replace the blown-out additive floor ring with a crisp, satin-painted colour band on the bevel of the grey base (plus a soft, non-white diffuse bounce on the wood), and shape `L3`/`L4` like translucent sculpted flame curling off the base rather than foggy sprites.
  3. **Fix billboard orientation and alpha blending on `L4`/`L5`**: Lock the `L4`/`L5` columns to the vertical Z-axis (cylindrical/axis-aligned billboards so they don't lean backward in high-angle views), fix the dark/sooty alpha fringes so particles fade purely additively, and render the `L5` emblem as a faint single-colour luminous silhouette rather than a shaded dark-lined illustration.
