### STEP 3: Evaluation Against Intent

#### a) Step-by-step progression & hardest pair to tell apart
* **Does it clearly increase step by step?** Mostly, **except between `L1` and `L2`**, which is by far the **hardest neighbouring pair to tell apart** in both day and night (especially from `table` and `high` angles).
* **Why `L1` vs `L2` fails at table distance**: `L1` has a crisp, saturated orange ring right at the base edge, while `L2`'s floor ring is softer/dimmer and its faint body haze blends into the warm orange floorboards. Unless you zoom in to notice that the `L2` miniature itself is lit warmer than `L1`, `L1` and `L2` look nearly identical in intensity.
* `L0→L1`, `L2→L3`, `L3→L4`, and `L4→L5` are all easy to tell apart.

* **Description**: A stylized golden **sun with a human face** inside a circular disk (heavy-lidded/closed eyes, brow, straight nose, and full lips) surrounded by a ring of alternating triangular pointed sun rays.

#### c) Six front-row colours

#### d) What looks broken
* **Visible tube edges (`L4`, `L5`)**: In `v7-low-day.png` and `v7-high-day.png`, the left and right edges of `L4` and `L5` form sharp, straight cylinder/cone walls against the wooden wall panels. In `high` views, the columns project upward-right like rectangular slabs/tubes.
* **Flat card look (`L5`)**: In `v7-low-day.png`, the sun emblem sits inside the column like a flat 2D decal/card.
* **Washing out the figure (`L4`, `silver`)**: On `L4`, the bright yellow-gold tint and additive streaks wash out the slender archer figure's face and silhouette. In `v7-low-day.png`, the front of the `silver` aura forms an opaque white patch hiding the figure's lower torso.
* **Clashing base rings / double-halo banding**: Every pawn retains a bright cyan selection/base border inside the coloured floor glow, creating a jarring two-tone neon ring (especially cyan inside red, gold, or violet).

#### e) Honest verdict: Cheap game effect vs. Painted-miniature-world effect
* **Verdict**: It looks like a **standard game VFX / MMO buff shader**, not a painted-miniature (OSL) effect.
* **What makes it look cheap/gamey**:
  1. **Translucent additive cylinders & billboard wisps**: Smooth alpha-blended vapour tubes (`L4`/`L5`) and particle wisps read immediately as digital shader geometry rather than physical miniature lighting.
  2. **Flat colour wash instead of sculpted OSL contrast**: Lit figures (`L2`–`L5`) get a uniform bright yellow tint rather than high-contrast directional highlights on raised edges with deep shadows in the recesses.
  3. **2D sticker emblem & cyan base rings**: The `L5` sun texture looks like a pasted 2D illustration, and the glowing cyan base rings break the illusion of coloured light coming from the pawn.
* **Three most important changes to move toward a painted-miniature look**:
  1. **Shift from floating additive smoke to true OSL on the mini and base**: Colour the pawn's base rim itself with the aura colour (replacing the cyan ring) and drive the effect with high-contrast directional rim/up-lighting on the miniature's normal map/geometry while keeping recessed shadows dark.
  2. **Eliminate the straight cylinder walls on `L4` and `L5`**: Taper upper columns inward or break them into sculpted, tapering flame ribbons so there are no straight vertical tube edges against the walls.
  3. **Separate `L1` and `L2` cleanly**: Keep `L1` as a subtle rim-only shimmer on the base bevel, and make `L2` strongly illuminate the lower half of the miniature with painted-style upward highlights so it pops even against the warm wood floor.

#### f) Comparison with `prev-table-day.png`
* **Slightly better (nearly the same at a glance)**:
  * **Why better**: In `prev-table-day.png`, the floor halos around the front-row bases were overly thick and blown out; `v7-table-day.png` tones down that harsh floor-ring blowout and gives the rising flame wisps (on `L3`–`L5` and the front row) slightly taller, clearer vertical structure.
  * **Why mostly the same**: The core issues—`L1` vs `L2` ambiguity, the cylindrical tube edges on `L4`/`L5`, and the digital billboard-shader look—are virtually unchanged.
