#### e) Honest verdict: Cheap Game Effect vs. Painted-Miniature-World Effect
* **Verdict**: **Cheap game effect** (looks like an MMO buff pillar + neon selection ring).
* **What makes it look cheap**:
  1. **Glowing empty air**: Tall additive light tubes (L4/L5) and neon floor rings glow in mid-air rather than interacting with physical surfaces.
  2. **Flat emissive wash on the mini**: Tinting the whole miniature uniformly flattens its shading, whereas real miniature Object Source Lighting (OSL) relies on high contrast between lit edges and deep shadows.
  3. **Blown-out neon base halos**: The floor rings saturate to pure white/neon against the wood grain.
* **Three most important changes toward a painted-miniature (OSL) look**:
  1. **Replace air glow with directional surface OSL**: Cut the additive air haze back drastically; put the colour into directional rim/up-lighting on the miniature's raised surfaces (preserving dark recesses/shadows so the face and sculpt stay crisp) and soft bounce light on nearby props/floorboards.
  2. **Ground the base ring into the physical base**: Instead of a wide neon halo bleeding onto the tavern floor, paint/illuminate the beveled rim and textured top of the grey base itself, with only a tight, low-intensity contact glow on the floor.
  3. **Give L4/L5 a tactile miniature form**: If L4/L5 must rise high above the pawn for tabletop readability, style the column and L5 emblem like translucent/painted resin spell effects or a sculpted back-banner/halo behind the figure (with its own shading and highlights) rather than an unshaded additive particle cylinder wrapping over the front of the mini.

#### f) Comparison with `before-table-day.png`
* **Slightly better, but fundamentally the same look.**
* **Why**: `v2` is **better** because the miniatures inside the auras (`gold`, `silver`, `blue`, `green`, and `L5`) now receive colored surface lighting on the 3D models themselves (in `before-table-day.png`, the front-row figures stood almost completely dark inside bright floor rings, and L5 was a washed-out pale ghost), and the L4/L5 columns in `v2` have a richer golden tone and cleaner sun emblem scaling than `before`'s chalky, noisy particle cloud. However, it still relies on the same neon floor discs and tall additive cylinders, and still suffers from the L1→L2 brightness drop.
