4. **Obscuring the miniature (L4, L5):** The front surface of the light cylinder sits between the camera and the miniature, washing the figure in monochrome yellow and veiling its painted face and armour details.

#### e) Honest Verdict: Cheap Game Effect vs. Painted-Miniature-World Effect
* **Verdict:** **Cheap game effect** (looks like an MMO/RTS cylindrical spell-buff VFX placed over a mini).
* **What makes it look cheap:**
  1. **Glowing geometry in empty air:** Translucent cylinders and flame cones hovering in mid-air around the figure break the physical miniature scale.
  2. **Neon coaster bases:** The entire top face of the grey base glows like an overexposed LED puck instead of a painted solid surface receiving light.
  3. **Lack of true Object-Source Lighting (OSL):** Real or painted miniature light is only visible where it hits solid surfaces (the folds of the mini, the textured base, the floorboards); here, almost all the brightness is in the air in front of the mini.
* **Three most important changes to move toward a painted-miniature-world look:**
  1. **Put the light on the miniature and floor, not in a front-facing cylinder:** Replace the translucent tube walls in front of the figure with actual directional/rim lighting (or a surface shader) on the miniature's 3D mesh and a cast light pool on the floorboards/walls—rising from ankle lighting (L1) to lower-body OSL (L2), full-body rim lighting (L3), and dramatic overhead/upward lighting with wide floor/wall spill (L4–L5).
  2. **Keep the grey base body solid and matte:** Stop flooding the top of the base disc with additive bloom; keep the grey base dark/solid so the figure's feet and the base name tag stay crisp, and place the glow either on the miniature's surfaces or as an under-base rim spill on the floorboards.
  3. **Replace the tall striped silo (L4/L5) with a miniature-scale backdrop/halo or floor projection:** Instead of a 2.5×-tall barcode tube, anchor high levels with a crisp painted-style halo/crest *behind* the figure's head/shoulders (so the face is never veiled) and project the L5 sun sigil sharply onto the floorboards or as a clean crest behind the mini.
