#### e) Honest Verdict: Cheap Game Effect vs. Painted-Miniature-World Effect
* **Verdict**: It currently reads as a **cheap MMO / video-game particle buff**, not a painted-miniature-world (OSL) effect.
* **What makes it look cheap**:
  1. **Additive fog in front of the figure lowers contrast**: Painted OSL works by *adding directional light and shadow contrast onto the miniature's sculpted surfaces*. Here, L4/L5 drop a milky yellow curtain *between* the camera and the miniature, flattening the mini into a washed-out silhouette.
  2. **Overblown radial floor splats**: The base rings bleach the floorboards to pure white/neon around the base instead of casting saturated colored bounce light onto the grey base rim and the figure's boots/cloak.
  3. **Scale-breaking laser tubes and a 2D bitmap stamp**: Straight-sided 15-foot vertical cylinders and a flat alpha-blended sun texture violate the physical scale and materiality of tabletop miniatures.
* **Three most important changes to achieve a painted-miniature look**:
  1. **Light the miniature's surfaces (true OSL / rim + upward point light) and stencil out front-facing fog**: Drive levels 2–5 primarily by illuminating the miniature's own normals (colored upward/rim lighting catching edges while leaving recesses dark) and depth-masking/carving out the aura in front of the torso and face so the painted figure is always the sharpest, highest-contrast element inside the glow.
  2. **Replace straight vertical billboard tubes (L3–L5) with compact, silhouette-hugging forms**: Shape rising energy like physical miniature effects (translucent sculpted resin flames or rising wisp geometry that hugs the base and arcs *behind* the shoulders/head, tapering off just above the figure rather than shooting to the ceiling as a cylinder).
  3. **Make the L5 emblem and base rings feel like physical tabletop materials**: Replace the flat 2D sun bitmap with a small, crisp, 3D relief-like halo/crest floating just behind/above the pawn's head (like a painted wargame icon or metallic/glowing crest), and confine the base glow to saturated pigment-style lighting on the grey base bevel and immediate floor without blowing out to white.
