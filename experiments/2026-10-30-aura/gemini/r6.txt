**e) Honest verdict: Cheap Game Effect vs. Painted-Miniature-World Effect**
* **Verdict:** **CHEAP GAME EFFECT.**
* **What makes it look cheap:**
  1. **Neon RTS/MMO floor rings and airbrushed fog shells:** Translucent glowing hula-hoops outside the base (`green`, `violet`, `red`) and soft additive smoke billboards floating in empty air belong to video game UI, not painted miniatures.
  2. **Lack of geometric Object Source Lighting (OSL):** Real miniature OSL lives *on the solid surfaces*—directional coloured rim-lighting on armor edges, cloth folds, weapon surfaces, and the textured top of the base, paired with deep contrasting shadows on the unlit sides.
  3. **Out-of-scale billboard pillars (L4/L5):** Towering translucent tubes with pixel sparks and a floating 2D sun image break the physical miniature scale.
* **Three most important changes to move toward a painted-miniature look:**
  1. **Move the light onto the miniature's geometry (true OSL):** Replace the mid-air fog shells on L1–L3 with strong, high-contrast directional rim/grazing light and specular highlights directly on the miniature's sculpted surfaces and the top bevel of the grey base.
  2. **Eliminate the neon outer ring around the base:** Instead of an additive halo outside the blue rim, illuminate the top surface of the grey base and cast a soft, wood-grain-respecting diffuse light pool onto the floorboards.
  3. **Give L3–L5 physical/sculpted forms:** Shape rising effects like painted translucent resin spell effects (crisp flame tongues hugging the base/back with painted edge highlights and darker recesses), and render the L5 emblem as a crisp painted halo/banner behind the miniature's head rather than a giant misty sky-tube.

* **Verdict:** **Slightly better** (though structurally very similar).
* **Why:**
  1. **L5 emblem is much cleaner:** In `prev-table-day.png`, the L5 emblem was a dark-shadowed, muddy relief near the top-right of the column that clashed with the brown wall; in `v6-table-day.png`, it is a clean, bright golden sun-face centered in the upper column.

  3. **Less washed-out front row:** The front-row auras in `prev-table-day.png` billowed higher and smokier over the figures' heads (`silver`, `blue`, `green`), washing out the miniatures more than the tighter `v6-table-day.png` version.
