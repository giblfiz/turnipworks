**Roof Description:**

1.  **Material:** The roofs are covered with thatch. I am very confident.

2.  **Relief:** The surface has real physical relief. The rows of thatch are modeled as separate, overlapping pieces with thickness. This is clear from the chunky, rounded silhouette at the eaves, the stepped profile at the gable ends, and the visible shading between the rows that gives them depth.

3.  **Continuity:** The rows run continuously across the main slopes. However, intersections are poor. In the valley where the two roofs of Building 1 meet, the geometry clips together jaggedly. The thatch also clips into the sides of the chimneys and the small dormer window without any proper fitting.

4.  **Style:** The roof fits the look of the walls, doors, and chimneys perfectly. Both have a consistent, chunky, stylized, and slightly simplified "game asset" look.

5.  **Broken/Wrong:**
    *   The valley on Building 1 is broken; the two roof planes clip through each other creating a jagged seam.
    *   On Building 2, the gable end has a large, untextured white triangle.
    *   There is a small, misplaced green sphere at the peak of the gable end on Building 2.
