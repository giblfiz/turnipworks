It is a representation of a shingled roof, not a physical construction of one.

2.  **Physicality and "Cheapness"**
    *   Roof **A** looks significantly more like a physical model of a roof. The three-dimensional relief of the individual shingles gives it depth and texture, mimicking how a miniature would be built.
    *   Roof **B** looks cheaper. The flat, repeating texture is a common shortcut in 3D modeling. It lacks depth, and the illusion breaks down immediately, making it look like a low-budget or older video game asset rather than a tangible model.
