## Boundaries and exceptions

No sibling collision follows from room/object Turn sharing, room/roof/building Re-roll sharing, object/roof Hide sharing, parent Indicators/leaf Star sharing, or parent/child menu reuse: the aliases occur in separate selection kinds or hierarchy levels. Toggle future-action states are one button at a time; the existing separate visual variants already cover them. Shape `as`/`shape` pairs are explicitly predicate-exclusive. `measure.keep`/`ruler.norounds`, `measure.attach`/`ruler.detach`, and `template.remove`/`template.clear` do not coexist as sibling strip buttons; no round2 paint required under the sibling-only rule.

**Unresolved runtime boundary:** Send-to-area can contain several identical hidden-area eye-off glyphs or public-area map fallbacks. No static action manifest can distinguish an unbounded set of campaign-defined area ids. Distinct runtime destination previews or generated badges would be required; this audit does not change game code. Existing unique public thumbnails remain an exemption only where present.

Conditions Clear is registered but lacks `menu:true` in current runtime. Painting it is explicitly requested round2 scope, not evidence that it currently appears in the Conditions flyout. Room type/Floor lists are cut from the ring; their chord/bar surfaces retain their existing image/preview mechanisms and are outside the ring sibling audit. Faction inherit, fog default, sheet tabs and numerical round input are text controls, not identical pictogram siblings.

Machine-readable co-visible IDs, file-collision groups, dynamic control keys and exemptions are in `round2-arcs.json`. Maximal outer-arc unions are conservative: actual permission/selection predicates can remove options.
