**1. LIST FIRST (view-1)**
* **Shapes:** Rounded, billowy, scalloped mounded clumps (cloud/broccoli-like lobes) with shaded crevices; irregular recessed depressions; tiny pinpoint specks/dots.
* **Colours:** Chartreuse, lime, and olive green with darker muted green shadows; warm ochre/brown in the depressions; tiny white, pale pink, and yellow dots.
* **Size and direction:** Clumps are roughly 1–2 ft across; shapes are omnidirectional/blobby with no linear flow, but form an evenly spaced, repeating grid rhythm across the 30 × 20 ft plane.

**2. BLIND NAME (view-1)**
* **Material & setting:** Living foliage / moss roof; rustic medieval or fantasy setting (e.g., Halfling/cottagecore).
* **Three words for a map key:** **Overgrown Moss Roof** (or **Living Turf Roof**)

**3. RENDERING (view-1)**
* **Style:** Chunky hand-painted brushwork (stylized digital paint with soft bevels and painted lighting, consistent with the hand-painted miniature aesthetic rather than procedural noise or photo textures).
* **5-ft repeat:** Yes. While seams are blended without hard lines, the identical placement of the brown dirt hollow and flower clusters in every 5-ft tile creates an obvious, distracting 6 × 4 repeating "wallpaper" grid at this distance.

**4. INTENT & VERDICT (view-1)**
* **Verdict:** **BAD**
* **Single biggest reason:** **Scale and form misread.** The puffy, bulbous, high-relief lobes read as the canopy of dense treetops, shrubbery hedges, or broccoli heads rather than a walkable, relatively flat sod/turf surface—an issue severely compounded by the noticeable 5-ft stamp repetition.
