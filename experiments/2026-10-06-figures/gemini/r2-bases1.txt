Here is a description and evaluation of the four variants.

**Description of what is under the figures:**

*   **base-dark:** Each figure stands on a solid, opaque, black circular disc of a noticeable thickness. There is no facing marker.
*   **base-rim:** Each figure stands on a solid black circular disc, identical in shape and thickness to the "dark" version. This disc has a glowing, reddish-pink rim around its vertical edge and a small, reddish-pink triangular marker on its top surface indicating direction.
*   **base-ring:** Each figure stands directly on the wooden floor. A thin, glowing, reddish-pink ring is drawn on the floor around the figure's feet. A small, reddish-pink triangular marker is integrated into the ring, pointing forward.
*   **base-none:** The figures stand directly on the wooden floor with nothing under or around them.

**Judgment as a player:**

1.  **Clearest as PIECES:** The `dark`, `rim`, and `ring` variants all clearly distinguish the figures as interactive game pieces. The `rim` and `ring` variants are slightly clearer due to the glowing effect, which acts as a modern user interface highlight. The `none` variant is the least clear, as the figures blend more into the scene like static decorations.

2.  **FACING marker:** The `rim` and `ring` variants have facing markers. In the `rim` variant, the small marker on the black base is very difficult to see at table distance. In the `ring` variant, the marker is part of the larger glowing ring on the floor, making it slightly more noticeable. In both cases, the marker correctly points in the direction the figure's body is facing.

3.  **Least harm to the figure's appearance:** The `ring` and `none` variants are best. The figures stand directly on the game floor, so their feet are not hidden. The `ring` is a simple, unobtrusive overlay. The `dark` and `rim` variants use a thick plinth that hides the figures' feet and makes them look less integrated into the environment.

4.  **Floating or sinking:** No figure appears to float or sink in any of the variants. All figures seem to be properly grounded on their respective surfaces (either the base or the floor).

5.  **Colour visibility:** The `ring` variant is significantly better for displaying a player colour. The colour is on a large, flat ring on the floor, which is fully visible from the isometric "table" view. In the `rim` variant, the colour is on the vertical edge of the base, which is mostly hidden from view at that angle.

BEST_OVERALL: ring
SECOND: dark
FACING_MARK_VISIBLE_AT_TABLE: barely
FLOATING_OR_SINKING: none
