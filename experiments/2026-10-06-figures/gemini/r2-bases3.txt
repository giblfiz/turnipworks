Here is a description and evaluation of the four variants.

### Description of what is under the figures

*   **base-dark:** Each figure stands on a solid, opaque, black circular disc. The disc is flat with a noticeable thickness, resembling a traditional physical miniature base. It completely covers the floor texture beneath the figure.
*   **base-rim:** Each figure stands on a transparent circular base that has a thick, solid, reddish-pink colored rim. The floor grid is visible through the transparent center. The rim has a small, triangular marker on one side to indicate the figure's facing direction.
*   **base-ring:** A thin, reddish-pink colored ring is drawn on the floor around each figure. The ring has no vertical thickness and the floor is fully visible inside it. The ring also has a small, triangular marker on one side to indicate facing.
*   **base-none:** There is nothing under the figures. They stand directly on the wooden floor, casting their own shadows onto the grid.

### Judgment as a player

1.  **Clarity as PIECES:** The **dark** variant is the clearest. The solid black bases are a classic and unambiguous signifier for a game piece, making the figures stand out distinctly from the static environment. The **rim** is a very close second, also clearly marking the figures as interactive pawns.

2.  **FACING:** The **rim** and **ring** variants both have facing markers. The marker on the **rim** is slightly easier to see because it's part of a thicker, more solid visual element. In both cases, the marker is visible at the "lean" distance and points in the same direction as the figure's body. At the "table" distance, the marker is quite small and difficult to see on the figures further away, but it is discernible on closer inspection.

3.  **Aesthetics (least intrusive):** The **ring** variant is the least intrusive of the options that provide a base. It clearly marks the figure's space on the grid without hiding their feet or the floor beneath them, and it doesn't look like a clumsy plinth because it has no thickness. The **none** variant is the most integrated into the scene, but it lacks any gameplay marker.

4.  **Floating or Sinking:** No figure appears to float or sink in any of the variants. The models are well-positioned on their respective surfaces (the base or the floor), and their feet appear to be firmly planted.

5.  **Color Visibility:** The **rim** variant would be far superior for color-coding. The thick, solid band of color is much more prominent and easier to identify at the "table" distance compared to the thin line of the **ring** variant.

BEST_OVERALL: rim
SECOND: ring
