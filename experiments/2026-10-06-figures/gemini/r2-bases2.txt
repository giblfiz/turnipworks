Here is a description and evaluation of the four variants.

**Description of what is under the figures:**

*   **base-dark:** Each figure stands on a solid, thin, circular black disc. There is no facing marker.
*   **base-rim:** Each figure stands on a solid, thin, circular black disc. The disc is outlined with a thick, glowing, reddish-orange rim. A small, triangular marker of the same color is on the rim, indicating the figure's forward direction.
*   **base-ring:** Each figure stands directly on the floor, and a thin, glowing, pinkish-red ring is drawn on the floor around them. A small, triangular marker of the same color is on the ring, indicating the figure's forward direction.
*   **base-none:** Each figure stands directly on the wooden floor. There is nothing else under or around them.

**Judgment as a player:**

1.  **Clarity as PIECES:** The **rim** variant is the clearest. The solid base and bright, glowing rim make the figures pop from the background and unambiguously identify them as active game pieces. The **ring** and **dark** variants are also very clear. The **none** variant is the least clear, as the figures could be mistaken for static scenery at a quick glance.
2.  **FACING:** The **rim** and **ring** variants both have a small triangular marker that accurately indicates which way the figure is facing. However, in both cases, the marker is only clearly visible in the close-up "lean" view. At the normal "table" distance, the marker is barely perceptible and not useful for quick assessment.
3.  **Aesthetics (hurts the figure least):** The **ring** variant is the best. Because the figure stands directly on the floor, it looks the most naturally integrated into the scene. The ring effectively marks the figure's space without creating a physical plinth that hides the feet or makes the figure look like it's on a display stand.
4.  **Floating or Sinking:** No figure appears to be floating above or sinking into its standing surface in any of the variants. The placement is consistently good across all images.
5.  **Color Visibility:** The **rim** variant has the most easily visible color at table distance. The rim is a thick, solid, high-contrast band of color that is much more prominent than the thinner line of the **ring** variant.
