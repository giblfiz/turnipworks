These photographs show one small figure from several sides (the file names say which: quarter, front, side, back). Describe it plainly; you are not told what it is meant to be.

1. What is it (best name, and a second guess)? What is it wearing? What is it holding, in which hand?
2. Go over it part by part and say what you see, and whether each part is well formed: head and face (eyes, nose, mouth), ears, each hand (can you tell fingers or a fist?), each foot or paw, tail if any, each thing it holds (is it complete end to end, straight, and of even thickness?).
3. List anything that looks malformed, fused together, webbed, melted, broken, missing, doubled, or floating free. Say exactly where. If nothing, say "nothing".
4. Is it painted in colour all over, or are there unpainted, black, grey or smeared patches? Where?
