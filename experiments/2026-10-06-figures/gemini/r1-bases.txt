Here is a description and evaluation of the four variants.

**Description of what is under the figures:**

*   **base-dark:** Each figure stands on a solid, opaque, black circular disc. The disc is flat and thin. On the front edge of the disc, there is a small, white, wedge-shaped marker indicating direction.
*   **base-rim:** Each figure is surrounded by a thick, glowing, reddish-orange rim. The area inside the rim is transparent, showing the wooden floor and grid lines beneath the figure. On the front edge of the rim, there is a small, white, wedge-shaped marker.
*   **base-ring:** Each figure is surrounded by a thin, glowing, pinkish-red ring. The area inside the ring is transparent, showing the floor and grid. On the front edge of the ring, there is a small, white, wedge-shaped marker.
*   **base-none:** There is nothing under the figures. They stand directly on the wooden floor, and there is no base or marker of any kind.

**Judgment as a tabletop role-playing game player:**

1.  **Clearest as PIECES:** The **dark** variant is the clearest. The solid black base is a traditional method for miniatures that unambiguously separates the figure from the environment, making it obvious it's a movable game piece. The **rim** is a close second, as the thick glowing border is also a very strong visual indicator.

2.  **FACING marker:** The **dark** variant shows the facing most clearly. The high contrast of the white marker on the solid black background makes it the most legible of the three that have it. The marker is visible at table distance in all three variants that have it, but it is very small and requires attention to spot. The marker correctly points the same way the figure's face and chest point in all applicable variants.

3.  **Hurts the figure least:** The **ring** variant is the least intrusive. It successfully marks the figure's occupied square without obscuring its feet or the ground beneath it, and its thinness prevents it from looking like a clumsy plinth. The figure feels well-integrated into the scene while still being clearly marked.

4.  **Floating or sinking:** In the **dark** variant, the figures' feet appear to sink slightly into the black disc, especially noticeable on the wizard's robes and the knight's sabatons in the "lean" view. The other variants show no floating or sinking.

5.  **Colour visibility:** The **rim** variant would make a player-specific colour easiest to see at table distance. Its thickness provides a large, bright surface area for the colour, making it much more prominent than the thin line of the **ring** variant.

BEST_OVERALL: ring
SECOND: rim

FLOATING_OR_SINKING: dark
