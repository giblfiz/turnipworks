# Nine faction families — plate work

54 requested native OpenAI imagegen plates, all 54 saved: 27 mesh sources and 27 flat decorations. Every family has idol, corbel, altarpiece, banner, relief and sigil. No optional sculptures, piers or motif-specific censers were generated; neutral censer reuse is intentional. No reference images were used. `../step2-assets.json` is the exact design spec; `source-provenance.json` records each exact prompt, source path and SHA-256. Mesh plate and mask gates live in the batch's `../plate-qa/` and are owned by the pipeline worker. All 27 flats passed blind 110px reads, then Codex contact-sheet inspection: `flat-gates.json`, `flat-qa/`, `flat-contact-sheet.jpg`. No flat rejections or rerolls.

The palettes are burnt copper/crimson (dragons), ivory/charcoal (skulls), violet/mauve (tentacles), moss/oak (nature), silver/royal blue (sword-shield), ivory/ink (respectable death), gold/emerald (scales-coin), iron/orange (forge-hammer), turquoise/pearl (sea-storm). Raven/hourglass and skull decoration are deliberately distinct. Blind readers identify the generic symbols, sometimes offering familiar mythology as a secondary association; prompts specified no named deity, published design or third-party reference. The 9 `flavor-altar-piece--…` SKU names retain their source-prompt spelling, but their canonical role/object is `flavor-altarpiece`.

`accepted-flats.pending.json` holds the 27 registration rows for integration; final native-alpha files are copied to `../flats/`. Do not activate before Step 1 lands. Flat material quality in-room, mesh structural QA, nave blind reads and live deployment remain downstream work, not claimed complete by these plate results. A 12-call concurrent generation attempt returned tool errors for 11 calls; retries in groups of 4–6 succeeded. No API fallback, extra credit spend or third-party image route was used.
