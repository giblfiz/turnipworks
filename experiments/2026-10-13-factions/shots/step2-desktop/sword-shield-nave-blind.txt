### What is Visible

#### Architecture and Structure
* **Layout:** A rectangular hall laid out on a grid with a checkered black-and-white tiled floor.
* **Walls & Openings:** Beige stone-block walls with dark quoins on the exterior corners. The front wall features a large arched wooden double door with iron hardware. Arched leaded/stained-glass windows are set into wall segments along the sides and behind the altar.
* **Sanctuary (Far End):** A raised stone dais with steps supporting a central stone altar. On the altar table sit several small vessels/bottles. Behind the altar stands a large sculptured crest.
* **Side Fixtures:** 
  * To the left of the altar is an elevated wooden preaching pulpit accessed by a small curved stair.
  * To the right of the altar stands a wooden reading lectern.
* **Seating:** Two columns of wooden pews/benches face the altar, separated by a central aisle. Each column contains five rows, alternating between longer back-supported pews and shorter/narrower wooden benches.
* **Entrance Clutter:** Beside the entrance doors sit small props: a multi-bottle rack and crate/chest on the left, and a barrel and small floor stand/disc on the right.
