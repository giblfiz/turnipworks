older library, already on the bench or aligned; listed here so the bench's lair pass has one page.

## The lair's own batch (lai-01, 2026-10-12)
No light, no vessel and no openable among the sixteen (the bone throne's two bone torches are unlit dressing, not a
light; the cave mouth is solid). The deploy's tool sweep lists all sixteen in the asset rater by itself.
Creature's floor: the kept-clear SPOT of each den is sized to the creature's BASE (10 ft: dragon, bear, owlbear; 5 ft:
spider), not to the drawn figure (figures draw at 0.67). A figure dropped on a den could snap to it (`lair.spot` on the
bed's placement).
