# Market & village: pieces for Harry's Alignment Bench

Tools: https://implausible.enterprises/projects/turnip-works/align/ (fire / water / open-shut). Three new pieces need an anchor; everything else the type uses was already on the earlier lists (the inn's hearths, ovens, lanterns and doors; `experiments/2026-10-05-castle-crypt-mansion/BENCH.md`).

| anchor | sku | what | note |
|---|---|---|---|
| fire | `lamp-post-iron--town` | the iron street lamp (squares, streets) | generated unlit; the flame goes inside the six-sided lantern at the top. `fireType()` already calls it a lantern |
| water | `pump-trough-village` | the village pump and its stone trough | generated dry: the water level is the trough's rim less a hand; a trickle at the spout if the Water panel grows one |
| water | `well-house` (library source outdoors-02, not this batch) | the roofed well the square may stand in its middle | the ingest sweep found it declared by nobody; named here so the bench lists it (this table is read as a cross-check) |
| hinge / open-shut | `cell-holding-wood--gate` | the lockup's barred gate (watch post) | a whole 5-ft panel with the door leaf in it, shut. No open twin was made: the hinge side and the leaf's rectangle want marking so the swing can be faked, or an open twin generated later |

Not on the bench, by design: the shop window (`wall-window-shop--half-timber`) is fixed glazing; the stalls' awnings and the wall awning do not move; the cradle and the spinning wheel are props.

Light sources the rooms use that were already benched (no new work): `fireplace-wall`, `bread-oven`, `lantern-wall-iron` / `-brass` / `-horn`, `candle-box-sconce`, `candlestick`, `torch-sconce`, `lamp-post-wood`, `lantern-post`.
