# Temple & chapel: pieces for Harry's Alignment Bench (2026-10-11)

Bench: https://implausible.enterprises/projects/turnip-works/align/

| piece | bench | why |
|---|---|---|
| `flavor-censer--sun` | fire | one fat candle in the ray dish at the top: where the flame sits |
| `flavor-censer--moon` | fire | one fat candle cradled in the crescent: where the flame sits |

Nothing else in this batch burns, holds water or opens. Not on the bench, and why:

- `confessional`: its doors and curtains are painted shut; no open twin was made.
- `flavor-sculpture--moon` (the moon basin): its "water" is a painted solid surface on purpose; put it on the water bench only if a live surface is wanted there.
- `bell-on-frame`: the bell does not swing.
- The fire types of every other light these rooms place (candle stands, sconces, torches, the sanctuary lamp) were set by earlier batches.
