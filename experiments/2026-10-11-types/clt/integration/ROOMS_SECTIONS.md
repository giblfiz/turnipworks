## Flavor slots (religious places: cult sanctum, temple; 2026-10-11)

Harry's flavor system re-dresses a religious place with a symbol-theme, never a named god. Evil-leaning: `devils`, `spiders`, `dragons`, `skulls`, `tentacles`. Good-leaning: `sun`, `moon`, `nature`, `sword-shield`. **The architecture and furniture of a sanctum or temple carry no iconography at all** (walls, altar, statues, robes, braziers, pews are neutral). Everything that says who is worshipped is one of EIGHT separable roles, the slots. One sku per flavor, named `<role>--<flavor>`; the flavor is the sku's `shape`.

| slot | role | what | place | how a room uses it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| altarpiece | `flavor-altarpiece` | the symbol-piece in the middle of the altar | mesh, on a surface | one per altar |
| idol | `flavor-idol` | the large statue of what is worshipped, on its own plinth | mesh, floor (scaled to stand under 9 ft walls) | centred on the end wall behind the altar; alone in an inner shrine |
| banner | `flavor-banner` | long hanging banner | FLAT, wall | on the long walls, in turn with the relief |
| relief | `flavor-relief` | the symbol as a carved tablet | FLAT wall card (a type with windows may fill the slot with a `flavor-window` kit panel) | on the long walls |
| sigil | `flavor-sigil` | the symbol large on the floor | FLAT, floor | inside the plain ritual circle, or alone before the altar or idol |
| censer | `flavor-censer` | standing candle-holder or censer | mesh, floor, a light (`fireType` → candle) | a pair flanking the altar or idol; **optional**: a flavor without one gets neutral tall candles |
| sculpture | `flavor-sculpture` | a second showpiece that is NOT the idol | mesh, floor | mirrored pairs in the corners of the end wall; every second pair of figures in a passage |
| buttress | `flavor-buttress` | a flying buttress in the flavor's motif (the vertical kit's `buttress` box) | mesh, against a wall | mirrored pairs down the long walls of a large hall (about 30 ft across or more) |

- **Code** (`js/solver2.js`, the block `// >>> types:flavor`, defined once and shared): `withFlavor(ctx, FLAVORS.evil | FLAVORS.good, forced)` picks the room's flavor and makes every roll of a `flavor-*` role in that room take that flavor's sku, whoever asks (a program, `mount()`, `rug()`, the shared `chapel()`). `flavorHas(ctx, role)` says whether this room's flavor fills a slot. **A slot the flavor has no sku for stays empty**: never another flavor's piece; the program names a neutral fallback where the room needs one (lights).
- **Which flavor a room takes**, in order: `room.flavor` (the flavor system, when it exists); `opts.flavor` (the tuner: `?flavor=spiders`); the room's **building** (the game: a seeded pick among the type's flavors that have an idol, so every room of one building agrees and buildings differ); else the first of the type's list (`devils`; a temple's would be `sun`). Rooms side by side never disagree by accident. A flavor asked for by name may cross the lists (a temple of spiders).
- **Data** (`build_data.py`): a flavor sku carries NO tier, whichever batch made it and whoever landed first; it is always asked for by shape.
- **Adding a flavor**: make the skus (`flavor-idol--dragons`, …), add nothing to any program. A flavor shows up in the game's seeded pick once its idol exists.
- **Adding a religious type**: call `withFlavor` at the top of each program (and of its annex), use the roles above, keep everything else neutral.
- Built: `devils`, `spiders` (cult sanctum batch); `sun`, `moon` (temple batch).

## Cult sanctum (nine room types, built 2026-10-11)

Wall family `black-stone` (near-black basalt ashlar with one dark red band; corners borrow dressed-stone pillars; slate roof); the hidden entrance is `dressed-stone`, because from outside it is a cellar. **No windows**, so every room ends with at least one light. Doors: black oak (`door-crypt`) inside and out; the hidden entrance has a plain cellar door to outside and to ordinary rooms, and **the bookcase** (`door-secret-bookcase`, a leaf) toward any other sanctum room. Programs: `solver2.js`, the appended `types:clt.solver2` block. Sanctum meshes of roles the library already had (the black pillar) are sku tier `cult`. All iconography is in the flavor slots above; nothing else in these rooms carries a symbol.

- **Hidden entrance** (4x3, passage): reads as an ordinary cellar store: shelves, barrels, crates and sacks thin along the walls, a lantern, cobwebs, sometimes a trapdoor. Nothing of the cult shows. Standing alone it still has its way on: the bookcase straight across from the cellar door (`THROUGH` + `WAY_ON any`).
- **Processional passage** (8x3, passage, entered at an end, a way on at the other): a clear line down the middle on the runner; against the long walls, mirrored pairs every 5 ft: hooded statues (every second pair of figures the flavor's sculpture) and fire bowls in turn; alcove panels (`wall-alcove`) in every second panel of both long walls, nothing tall before them; banners and reliefs on the plain panels. Under 9 ft across: the walls and their lights only.
- **Sanctum** (5x8, terminal, door on a short wall): the idol centred on the end wall (rooms 19 ft deep or more); the altar slab on the axis before it, lying across the axis where the room is wide enough, with the altar-piece, a candle toward each end and one ritual thing (dagger, chalice or bowl); a censer each side; the plain ritual circle with the sigil inside it on the axis before the altar, its floor kept clear; a fire bowl each side of the circle; at most four rows of seats behind the circle either side of the aisle; pillars against the long walls in mirrored pairs from 19 ft across, the flavor's buttresses in their place from 29 ft; sculptures (else hooded statues) in the corners of the end wall; banners and reliefs down the long walls.
- **Summoning chamber** (5x5, terminal): the plain circle with the sigil dead centre (the sigil alone in a small room); a boundary stone at each of its four points; tall candles at the two side points; the empty arch centred on the wall facing the door; the chained book on its lectern at the circle's edge, off the door's line, turned to the circle; fire bowls in the far corners; a work table by the door.
- **Holding cells** (5x4, terminal, dark): cages in a row along the wall facing the door, one per 6 ft; a bucket; manacles on the side walls; a pallet; the gaoler's table, stool and keys by the door; ONE fire.
- **Robing room** (4x3, uses the sanctum): a rail of hooded robes standing in the open along the room's long axis (two from 20 cells; against the far wall in a small room); peg rails of robes on the walls; a bench, chests, a wardrobe; a table with blank masks and a folded robe; somewhere to wash by the door.
- **Forbidden library** (4x4, terminal): the chained book on its lectern in the middle of the floor facing the door (on the far wall in a small room), a tall candle each side; bookcases side by side, the wall facing the door first, until half the wall length is books; a reading table with the strapped tomes, the chained book, a black candle, a skull.
- **Acolytes' quarters** (5x4, terminal): pallets (bunks from 35 cells) heads to the wall, one per 4.5 ft, down both long walls where two fit with a way between; a chest at each foot where 4 ft is left to walk; robes on pegs by the door; a small table. No arms.
- **Inner sanctum** (3x4, terminal): the idol centred on the wall facing the door; the sigil on the floor before it and the offering bowl on it; a censer each side; the open treasure chest in a door-wall corner, chests on the side walls, pedestals with a tome or a chalice; a sculpture and a fire bowl in the far corners from 20 cells. One vault in three has the high priest's secret door standing ajar (`wall-secret-door`, a panel).
- **The leg of an L-shaped room** gets walls, lights and the flavor's signs, never a second key piece.
