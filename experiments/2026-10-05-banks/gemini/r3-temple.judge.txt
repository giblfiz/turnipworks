1.  **High table-distance view:** 6/10. The overall shape is coherent, but the grid-like seams between the constituent pieces are visible upon inspection, revealing its modular nature.
    **Low close view:** 3/10. The seams and hard edges between the pieces are much more obvious from this angle, making it read more like separate, ill-fitting blocks.

2.  The following seams are visible:
    *   **Between the central ramp and the side slopes:** A hard, dark vertical line is visible on both sides of the ramp where it meets the main hill. This is **glaring** in the low view and **noticeable** in the high view.
    *   **Between the straight slopes and the corner pieces:** There are clear vertical seams where the rounded corner sections connect to the straight flank sections on all four corners. These are **noticeable** in both views.
    *   **Along the top edge:** The transition from the flat top to the sloped sides is a perfectly sharp, geometric edge, which reads as an artificial cut rather than a natural contour. This is **noticeable** in both views.

3.  The grass colours do not perfectly match.
    *   **Hue:** The green hue is very similar and consistent.
    *   **Brightness:** The slopes appear slightly darker than the flat ground, though this could be partially due to lighting.
    *   **Texture Scale:** This is the main mismatch. The grass texture on the slopes is visibly stretched, blurred, and lower in resolution compared to the crisp, detailed texture on the flat ground. The distortion of the grid lines on the slope makes this obvious.

4.  The overall shape reads as geometric and man-made, not a believable natural terrain. The single biggest thing that breaks the illusion is its **unnatural geometric regularity**: the perfectly flat top, the uniform slope angles, and the straight-edged, near-square footprint.

5.  Looking at the pawns on the raised land:
    *   **Yellow pawn:** Sits convincingly on the flat top surface.
    *   **Blue pawn:** Cuts into the slope. The back of its round base is visibly submerged into the terrain.
    *   **Red pawn:** Cuts into the slope of the ramp.
    *   **White figure:** Sits mostly convincingly, but its base appears to slightly clip into the ground on its left side.

6.  **At the high table-distance view only:** Mostly, because its gameplay function as an elevated area is immediately obvious, and players tend to accept such abstractions for the sake of play.

VERDICT: mostly continuous with visible seams
