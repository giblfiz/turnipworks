1.  **High table-distance view:** 4/10. The overall form is coherent, but the straight edges and distinct corner pieces make it look like a set of modular terrain blocks assembled together.
    **Low close view:** 2/10. From this angle, the seams between the pieces are much more obvious, and it clearly reads as separate geometric objects placed on the ground and next to each other.

2.  *   **Top edge of the cliff:** The line where the vertical rock face meets the horizontal grass top is a perfectly straight, hard edge. This is **glaring** in the low view.
    *   **Between cliff sections:** There are vertical seams between the straight cliff faces and the rounded corner pieces. This is most **noticeable** on the right side in the high-angle view.
    *   **Between cliff and ramp:** Where the rocky cliff section on the right ends and the grassy slope on the left begins, there is a clear vertical seam where two different objects meet. This is **glaring** in the low view.
    *   **Base of the landform:** The entire base of the raised area has a hard, visible edge where it meets the flat ground grid, reinforcing that it is an object sitting on top of the surface, not rising out of it. This is **noticeable**.

3.  The grass colours do not perfectly match.
    *   **Hue:** The green hue is a very close match.
    *   **Brightness:** The grass on the slopes is slightly darker than the flat ground, which is believable due to lighting and angle.
    *   **Texture Scale:** The texture on the slopes is visibly stretched and lower resolution than the texture on the flat ground. The grassy/mossy details are larger and blurrier on the raised landform.

4.  The shape reads as geometric and man-made, like a piece of wargaming terrain, not a natural landform. The single biggest thing that breaks the illusion is the hard, unnaturally straight top edge of the cliff face. Natural erosion does not create such a perfect, sharp ninety-degree angle between a vertical cliff and a flat top.

5.  *   **Blue pawn:** Its base sits convincingly on the flat top surface.
    *   **Red pawn:** Its base is not sitting flat. It is positioned on the start of the slope, causing the back edge of the circular base to float slightly above the ground.

6.  **At the high table-distance view only:** Mostly. A player would accept it because its function as a raised cliff is immediately clear, even if its form is not naturalistic.

VERDICT: Mostly continuous with visible seams.
