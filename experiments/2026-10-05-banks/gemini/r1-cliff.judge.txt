**3. Grass Colour and Texture Match**
The grass colours do not match.
*   **Hue:** The grass on the raised landform is a duller, more olive-toned green. The flat ground is a more vibrant, saturated green.
*   **Brightness:** The hill's grass is darker overall than the flat ground.
*   **Texture Scale:** The textures do not match. The flat ground has a fine, detailed grass/flower texture appropriate for the grid scale. The grass on the hill is a larger, blurrier, and more repetitive texture.

**4. Shape Believability**
The shape reads as geometric and man-made, not natural.
*   **The single biggest thing that breaks the illusion is the perfectly square, 90-degree corner block on the right side.** Natural erosion does not create such forms; it looks like a pre-fabricated game piece.

**5. Pawn Placement on the Surface**
*   **Red pawn:** Sits convincingly. It is on a flat section of the top, and its base appears flush with the ground.
*   **Blue pawn:** Does not sit convincingly. It is on a slope. The base remains perfectly horizontal, causing the back edge to float slightly above the ground while the front edge appears to clip into the surface.
