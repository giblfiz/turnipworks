1.  **High table-distance view:** 6/10. The overall shape is coherent, but the hard edges and straight lines suggest it's constructed from pieces.
    **Low close view:** 3/10. The seams and unnatural vertical faces are much more obvious, making it look like separate, poorly-fitted objects.

2.  The following seams and hard edges are visible:
    *   **Location:** The vertical join between the sheer pinkish-brown cliff face on the left and the sloped grassy corner next to it. **Noticeability:** Glaring. It's a perfectly straight vertical line.
    *   **Location:** The top edge of that same sheer cliff face. **Noticeability:** Glaring. It is a sharp, unnatural 90-degree angle.
    *   **Location:** The join between the straight bank section and the rounded corner on the right side of the hollow. **Noticeability:** Noticeable. A clear vertical line is visible where the two pieces meet.
    *   **Location:** The inner corner of the hollow, where the two sloped sides meet. **Noticeability:** Subtle. A faint line suggests two modular terrain pieces meeting at a corner.

3.  The grass colours do not fully match.
    *   **Hue and Brightness:** The hue and general brightness are consistent between the flat ground and the slopes.
    *   **Texture Scale:** The texture scale does not match. On the steeper slopes, particularly the inner curve of the hollow, the grass texture is visibly stretched vertically. This makes the details on the slope look smeared and elongated compared to the crisp texture on the flat ground.

4.  The overall shape reads as geometric and man-made, not as natural terrain. The single biggest thing that breaks the illusion is the perfectly straight, sheer, vertical cliff face on the left. Natural erosion does not create such clean, planar surfaces joined at sharp angles.

5.  Looking at the pawns on the raised land:
    *   **Red pawn:** Its base cuts into the slope. The uphill side of the round base is buried in the grass, while the downhill side floats slightly above it.
    *   **Blue pawn:** Its base sits convincingly on the surface, which is relatively flat at that spot.
    *   **White figure:** Its base sits convincingly on the surface.
    *   **Pink pawn:** Its base floats slightly above the ground. There is a visible gap under the edge of the base closest to the sheer drop.

6.  **At the high table-distance view only:** Mostly. A player would accept it as functionally representing a raised area because its purpose on a grid-based map is clear, even if it looks artificial.

VERDICT: separate objects.
