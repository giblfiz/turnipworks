1.  **High table-distance view:** 7/10. From this distance, it mostly reads as a single landform, but its regular, somewhat geometric outline hints at its artificial nature.
    **Low close view:** 3/10. From this angle, it clearly reads as a separate, pre-fabricated object placed on top of the flat ground plane.

2.  The most prominent seam is the entire perimeter where the base of the raised landform meets the flat ground. In the low view, this is a **glaring** hard edge, like a model placed on a board. In the high view, it is **noticeable**. There is also a **subtle** horizontal seam visible in the high view running across the front of the hill, just below the level of the blue pawn, suggesting the hill itself is constructed from stacked horizontal sections.

3.  The grass colours do not perfectly match.
    *   **Hue:** The green hue is very similar and matches well.
    *   **Brightness:** The slopes are slightly darker than the flat ground, which could be attributed to shadow, but the base texture itself appears to have a lower value.
    *   **Texture Scale:** The texture on the slopes is visibly lower resolution and appears stretched or scaled up compared to the crisp, finer texture on the flat ground. This is the most significant mismatch.

4.  The overall shape reads as geometric and man-made. The single biggest thing that breaks the illusion is the unnaturally uniform and smooth slope around the entire perimeter. A natural hill would have more variation, with gentler and steeper sections, divots, and a more irregular footprint. This looks like a 3D model created with a simple extrusion.

5.  *   **Yellow pawn:** Its base cuts into the slope at the back.
    *   **Blue pawn:** Its round base cuts deeply and unconvincingly into the slope.
    *   **White figure:** It stands on a flatter terrace and its base sits convincingly on the surface.

6.  **At the high table-distance view only:** Yes, a player would accept this as "a hill" because it clearly and effectively communicates a change in elevation with sloped, difficult terrain, which is its primary gameplay function.

VERDICT: Separate objects.
