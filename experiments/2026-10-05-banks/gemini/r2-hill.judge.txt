1.  **Continuity Score:**
    *   **High table-distance view:** 6/10. It forms a single overall shape, but the modular nature is apparent from the unnatural creases and grid alignment.
    *   **Low close view:** 3/10. The seams and the blocky nature of the individual pieces are much more obvious and distracting.

2.  **Visible Seams and Edges:**
    *   **Location:** The V-shaped valley running between the yellow and light-blue (center) pawns. **Noticeability:** Glaring, especially in the low view. The grid lines clearly misalign.
    *   **Location:** The crease running down from the right of the red pawn towards the blue pawn. **Noticeability:** Noticeable. It's an unnaturally straight line for a terrain feature.
    *   **Location:** The front face of the piece the central light-blue pawn is on. It has a hard, defined, almost rectangular edge facing the viewer in the low-angle shot. **Noticeability:** Noticeable.
    *   **Location:** The entire outer perimeter of the landform, particularly the rounded corners, reads as the edge of a manufactured piece rather than a natural slope blending into the ground. **Noticeability:** Subtle to noticeable.

3.  **Grass Colour and Texture Match:**
    *   **Hue and Brightness:** The hue and base brightness of the grass texture on the slopes match the flat ground perfectly. The shading on the slopes is a natural consequence of lighting.
    *   **Texture Scale:** The texture scale does not match. The grid texture is severely stretched and distorted on the sloped surfaces, whereas it is uniform on the flat ground. This is a major inconsistency.

4.  **Overall Shape Believability:**
    The shape reads as geometric and man-made, specifically like modular wargaming terrain tiles. It is not believable as a single natural terrain feature.
    *   **Single biggest illusion-breaker:** The unnaturally straight and uniform creases where the modular pieces meet. Natural erosion does not create such regular valleys and ridges.

5.  **Pawn Placement on Raised Land:**
    *   **Red pawn:** Sits convincingly on a relatively flat peak.
    *   **Yellow pawn:** Cuts into the slope on its left side, floats slightly above the surface on its right.
    *   **Light-blue pawn (center):** Cuts significantly into the slope on its left, floats noticeably above the surface on its right.
    *   **Purple pawn:** Sits convincingly. The slope is gentle enough that any clipping or floating is negligible.
    *   **Light-blue pawn (lower):** Cuts into the surface. The base is visibly sunken into the grass texture.

6.  **Player Acceptance (High View):**
    Mostly. A player would accept it because its function as elevated terrain providing cover and line-of-sight blocking is immediately obvious, even if its appearance is artificial.

VERDICT: mostly continuous with visible seams
