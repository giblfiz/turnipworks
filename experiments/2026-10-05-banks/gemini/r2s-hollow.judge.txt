1.  **High table-distance view:** 8/10. From this distance, the landform largely reads as a single, continuous piece, with the shadows and highlights creating a cohesive shape.
    **Low close view:** 3/10. From this angle, it clearly reads as separate objects assembled together. The seams and hard edges are obvious and break the illusion of a single, natural form.

2.  The following seams and hard edges are visible, primarily in the low-angle view:
    *   **Location:** The straight, vertical section on the left (which looks like a wooden fence or palisade) has a hard, straight seam where it meets the curved earthen bank next to it. **Noticeability:** Glaring.
    *   **Location:** The entire inner rim of the hollow, where the top flat surface meets the inner slope. This is a perfectly sharp, continuous edge that looks like a clean cut. **Noticeability:** Glaring.
    *   **Location:** The outer top rim of the landform. Similar to the inner rim, it's an unnaturally sharp and level edge, suggesting it's a separate top piece placed on a sloped base. **Noticeability:** Noticeable.
    *   **Location:** In the high view, a subtle vertical line is visible on the outer bank just to the left of the purple pawn, suggesting a join between two curved pieces. **Noticeability:** Subtle.

3.  The grass colours mostly match.
    *   **Hue and Brightness:** The hue and general brightness are consistent between the flat ground, the slopes, and the top of the landform. The lighting creates natural variations in brightness.
    *   **Texture Scale:** The texture scale does not match. On the steeper slopes, particularly the inner ones, the grass texture appears stretched vertically. It does not conform to the terrain realistically, unlike the texture on the flat ground and the top surface, which appears correct.

4.  The overall shape reads as geometric and man-made, not a believable natural terrain. While it resembles a crater or atoll in concept, the execution is too clean. The single biggest thing that breaks the illusion is the unnaturally sharp, perfectly level, and continuous top edge of the rim, which looks manufactured rather than eroded by nature.

5.  Looking at the pawns on the raised land (best seen in the low view):
    *   **Yellow pawn:** Sits convincingly on a flat section of the top surface.
    *   **Blue pawn:** Sits convincingly on a flat section of the top surface.
    *   **Red pawn:** Sits on the inner rim and its base cuts slightly into the slope.
    *   **Purple pawn:** Sits on the outer slope and its base cuts deeply and unconvincingly into the hillside.

6.  **At the high table-distance view only:** Mostly. A player would accept it as "a hill" because the overall shape and the way the grid deforms over it clearly communicate a change in elevation, which is sufficient for gameplay at a glance.

VERDICT: Mostly continuous with visible seams.
