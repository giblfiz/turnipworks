1.  **High table-distance view:** 4/10. The overall shape is held together, but the straight, hard-edged banks and the unnaturally sharp rim of the central depression make it look like a collection of pre-fabricated, modular pieces.
    **Low close view:** 7/10. This angle is more forgiving. The slopes blend better and it reads more like a single landform, though some artificial edges are still visible.

2.  The following seams and unnatural edges are visible:
    *   **Location:** The long, straight, vertical bank on the left side, below the pink pawn. **Noticeability:** Glaring. It looks like a completely separate, man-made retaining wall piece with a different texture and colour.
    *   **Location:** The inner rim of the central depression. **Noticeability:** Noticeable. The edge is too sharp and uniform, looking like two 3D models intersecting rather than a natural slope.
    *   **Location:** The short, straight bank on the right side, near the yellow pawn in the low-angle view. **Noticeability:** Noticeable. It's another hard, geometric edge that breaks the natural illusion.
    *   **Location:** The transition from the flat top (where the red and blue pawns are) to the surrounding slopes. **Noticeability:** Subtle. The edge is a bit too crisp, like a crease, rather than a soft, eroded hilltop.

3.  The grass colours do not perfectly match.
    *   **Hue:** The hue of the green is consistent between the flat ground and the raised land.
    *   **Brightness:** The brightness is consistent, accounting for natural shadows and highlights from the lighting.
    *   **Texture Scale:** The texture scale does not match. The grass texture on the slopes is visibly stretched, blurry, and lower-resolution compared to the crisp, detailed texture on the flat ground. This is most obvious on the larger, smoother slopes.

4.  The overall shape reads as geometric and man-made. While it has some mounds and slopes, the combination of a hollowed-out center and several perfectly straight cliff faces is not believable as a natural formation. The single biggest thing that breaks the illusion is the long, straight, pinkish-brown vertical bank on the left; it looks like a modular terrain piece that was not blended into the scene.

5.  *   **Red pawn:** Sits convincingly on the surface.
    *   **Blue pawn:** Sits convincingly on the surface.
    *   **Yellow pawn:** Cuts into the slope on its upper side and floats slightly above the surface on its lower side.
    *   **Pink pawn:** Cuts into the slope significantly on its right side and floats noticeably above the surface on its left side.

6.  **At the high table-distance view only:** Mostly, because it clearly communicates a change in elevation on a game grid, which is its primary function.

VERDICT: Mostly continuous with visible seams.
