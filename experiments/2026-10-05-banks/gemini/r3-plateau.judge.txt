1.  **High table-distance view:** 6/10. From a distance, the overall shape holds together, and the props (rocks, bushes) help disguise the construction. However, the major seams are still visible.
    **Low close view:** 3/10. From this angle, the hard edges and seams between the modular pieces are starkly obvious. It clearly reads as separate, fitted objects.

2.  The following seams and hard edges are visible:
    *   A seam runs vertically down the front face of the landform, starting directly between the blue and yellow pawns. It is **glaring** in the low view.
    *   Another seam runs from the top surface down the left slope, curving towards the lone tree. It is **noticeable** in both views.
    *   A seam is visible on the right slope, running down from near the yellow pawn. It is **noticeable**.
    *   The entire perimeter where the base of the raised landform meets the flat ground is a hard, sharp, continuous edge. This reads as an exposed cut face and is **glaring** from all angles.

3.  The grass colours and textures do not fully match.
    *   **Hue:** The green hue is consistent between the slopes and the flat ground.
    *   **Brightness:** The slopes are darker than the flat ground. This is partially due to lighting angles, which is natural, but the base texture itself also appears slightly darker.
    *   **Texture Scale:** The texture scale does not match. The grid lines are stretched and distorted on the curved slopes, whereas they are uniform squares on the flat ground. This is also true for the small patches of brown/yellow dirt texture.

4.  The shape is not believable as natural terrain; it reads as a geometric, man-made game piece. The single biggest thing that breaks the illusion is the perfectly clean, hard edge where the base of the landform meets the flat ground. Natural terrain would have a gentle, blended slope, scree, or at least an irregular, broken edge, not a uniform, sharp line.

5.  Looking at the pawns on the raised land:
    *   **Blue pawn:** Its base cuts into the slope. The front edge of the round base is visibly submerged beneath the grass texture.
    *   **Yellow pawn:** Its base sits convincingly on the surface. It is on a relatively flat part of the crest and appears flush with the ground.

6.  **High table-distance view only:** Mostly. A player would accept it because its function as "elevated terrain" is immediately obvious for gameplay, despite the visible construction flaws.

VERDICT: mostly continuous with visible seams
