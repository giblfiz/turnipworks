#### Judgment
1. **Can each top face be read at this distance?**: **Yes**. Numerals `20`, `40`, and `4` are large, well-contrasted, and readable.
2. **Do Hunger dice read as different from regular ones?**: N/A.
3. **Hope vs Fear distinct?**: N/A.
4. **Can a player tell success, opportunity, strife, explosive apart?**: N/A.
5. **Anything broken?**:
   * **Wrong colours / visual ambiguity**: All three dice share the identical light blue body material. In Call of Cthulhu bonus/penalty rolls, the bonus/penalty tens die must be visually distinguished from the primary tens die by color (e.g., green for bonus tens, red for penalty tens, or distinct colorway). * **Verdict**: **FIX**
  * *Concrete Fix*: Apply a distinct material/color shader to bonus and penalty tens dice in CoC percentile rolls (e.g., emerald green for bonus, dark charcoal/red for penalty) so they do not clone the roller's base blue die.
