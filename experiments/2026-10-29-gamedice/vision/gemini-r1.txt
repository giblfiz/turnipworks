### 2. Evaluative Judgments

#### (1) Can the top face be read on each die at this distance?
* **Fate (`gamedice-fate-forced.png`):** **Yes.** The cream glyphs (`+`, `−`, blank) are bold, high-contrast, and instantly identifiable at table distance.
* **CoC (`gamedice-bar-coc.png`):** **Partially.** Numbers are crisp and aided by tag pills, but reading them is impeded because the tens digits are oriented upside-down (`50` looks like `05`, `60` looks like `09`) and the units die is sideways.
* **Duality (`gamedice-duality-forced.png`):** **No.** While the gold Hope die's `11` is clear, the Fear die is caught under the dark announce banner and its pale lilac numerals on dark purple have poor legibility through the card.
* **V5 (`gamedice-v5-forced.png`):** **No.** Only the blue `7` is visible; the remaining 5 dice (including both hunger dice) are blocked by the central announce card.
* **L5R (`gamedice-l5r-forced.png`):** **No.** The entire dice cluster is hidden behind the announce card; top faces cannot be read at all.
* **Shadowrun (`gamedice-bar-sr.png`):** **No.** 11 dice are compressed into an intersecting geometric mass where the faces collide into an illegible scribble.

#### (2) Do the Hunger dice read as clearly different from regular dice?
* **Color:** **Yes.** The deep crimson body color (`#a3161a`) sharply contrasts with the player's soft blue (`#67aadd`).
* **Faces:** **No in current shots.** Because the hunger dice are occluded behind the announce card in `gamedice-v5-forced.png`, the custom claw (`1`) and fang (`10`) symbols cannot be seen on the 3D dice at table distance.

#### (3) Do Hope and Fear dice read as clearly different from each other?
* **Yes.** The materials and colors are distinctly opposed: Hope is warm, lustrous gold (`#d8a62c`) with dark serif ink; Fear is deep shadowy violet (`#3b2357`) with pale lilac ink (`#ead9ff`). They are immediately distinguishable at a glance.

#### (4) Are the symbols distinct from each other at small size (Success, Opportunity, Strife, Explosive)?
* **Sheet Level:** **Yes.** The silhouettes are distinct: spearhead vs. rounded flower vs. jagged crack vs. rayed spearhead.
* **Table Distance on Dice:** **Questionable for Explosive vs. Success & Pairs.** At table distance on a d12 facet (especially on compound faces like `E+T` or `S+O` where each symbol is reduced to 50% cell size), the eight thin radiating triangular rays of `sym-explosive` become tiny single-pixel spikes that blur into the spearhead contour. The floating tag pills (`[ O+T ]`, `[ S+T ]`, etc.) are doing nearly all the heavy lifting for legibility on compound faces.

#### (5) Any face that looks broken, blank when it should not be, clipped, or wrongly coloured?
* **Broken / Geometry Collapse:** In `gamedice-bar-sr.png`, 11 of 12 d6s share identical spawn coordinates, clipping into a single fluted column with z-fighting top faces.
* **Clipped / Occluded by UI:** In all four `*-forced.png` images, the `#announce .ann` card sits at `left: 50%; top: 42%`, directly in front of the camera focus point (`close_shot`), blocking the roll.
* **Wrongly Oriented:** In `gamedice-bar-coc.png`, both d10t tens faces are oriented upside-down (180° rotated relative to camera) and the d10 units face is rotated 90°.
* **Blank Faces:** Fate's blank faces and L5R's blank faces are correct according to game rules.

#### (6) Do any symbols resemble a published game's official dice glyphs?
* **No (Pass).** The symbols are distinct, original vector designs:
  * V5 avoids White Wolf's proprietary ankh and skull marks, using three claw slashes and two fangs with a drop.
  * L5R avoids FFG's proprietary mon/kamon emblems (spiral strife mon, double-ring opportunity mon), substituting a Western spearhead, a five-petal cherry blossom, and a jagged crack.
  * Fate uses standard mathematical `+` and `−`.
  * Daggerheart uses standard Arabic numerals on gold/violet dice.

### 3. Verdict per Image

1. **`gamedice-symbols-sheet.png`**
   * **Verdict:** **PASS**
   * **Note:** Clean, original, distinct iconography with strong silhouettes and clear semantic distinction.

2. **`gamedice-v5-forced.png`**
   * **Verdict:** **FIX**
   * **Concrete Fix:** Dismiss or hide the `#announce .ann` popup before taking the camera shot in `browser_gamedice.py` (or wait for announce to settle) so the card does not occlude the dice pool; add initial spawn scatter jitter so the 6 d10s don't cluster tightly under the UI center.

3. **`gamedice-l5r-forced.png`**
   * **Verdict:** **FIX**
   * **Concrete Fix:** Hide `#announce` before `close_shot`; fix the tag pill positioning bug that caused `[ O+T ]` to float away over the chandelier.

4. **`gamedice-fate-forced.png`**
   * **Verdict:** **FIX**
   * **Concrete Fix:** Dismiss `#announce` before screenshot capture so the center `−` die and its tag pill are not occluded by the banner card.

5. **`gamedice-duality-forced.png`**
   * **Verdict:** **FIX**
   * **Concrete Fix:** Dismiss `#announce` prior to capture; increase the brightness/contrast of the lilac ink on `duality-d12-fear` (e.g. lighten from `#ead9ff` to near-white `#f5eeff`) to ensure legibility on the dark violet body at table distance.

6. **`gamedice-bar-sr.png`**
   * **Verdict:** **FIX**
   * **Concrete Fix:** In `tools/spawn-tuner/js/fx/dicephys.js` / `tw.roll`, add randomized lateral spawn offsets (`dx`, `dz`) and initial separation velocity when throwing pools > 6 dice to prevent physics co-location and interpenetration collapse.

7. **`gamedice-bar-coc.png`**
   * **Verdict:** **FIX**
   * **Concrete Fix:** In `tools/spawn-tuner/js/fx/dicegeo.js` (`remap`), correct the face `up` orientation vector for `d10` and `d10t` so top face decals face upright toward the player camera (-z) rather than inverted 180° or rotated 90°.

---

### Overall Verdict

**FIX — The original symbol art, skin palettes, and mechanical tallies are well-crafted, but table-distance verification fails due to announce card camera occlusion, inverted d10 face decals, and large-pool physics interpenetration.**
