## Big pools (branch `bigpool`, worktree ../3d-vtt-bigpool)

- 07:35 Finding: the forced-face search was never the cost. dicegeo.remap already turns each die's numbering so the face that lands up reads the committed number. The tries are the all-or-nothing REJECTION: one die cocked on a table edge, off screen or on another rejects the whole handful, so a clean landing falls as p^N. Captured the real browser scene (furnished common room 8x6, the AI path from the bottom of the view, Harry's dev house dice: size 3, bounce 0.85, reach 30, tries 15) and replayed it in node: p is about 0.8 a die; 12 dice and up never landed, every try went to the flat drop. In the browser at the house look (swiftshader, box load 30-58) base 12d6 took 6.4-7.8 s (always the flat drop, so "no dice" at 2.5 s); 20d6 9-12 s; 30d6 none at all (MAX_DICE was 24).
- Tried and dropped: (1) hand-by-hand, each hand of 4 simulated against the hands before as kinematic movers: no better than p^N (later hands batted off by the kinematic dice in the air); (2) keep the clean dice but also drop every die a bad one touched: kept nothing (on the floor a big pool is one contact cluster); (3) let re-thrown dice pass kept ones in the air (collision groups, then per-pair hooks): dice ended inside each other, or were shoved at 20 ft/s when the groups switched mid-overlap.
- Chosen: dicephys BIG POOLS. More than BIG_POOL.over = 8 dice (up to 8 is byte-identical to before; the tuner's D4 is unchanged: 2.49 tries, 2 flat drops of 300): the same handful and dials, judged die by die; clean dice are kept with their paths, the rest are thrown again from their places in the handful 0.25 s later per round (at most 4 handfuls), against the kept dice as kinematic movers, aimed at open floor among them; then 2 gentler rounds, then the flat drop for what is left. Recording `r` = when each die leaves the hand; playback does not draw a die before (fx/dice.js; the game's fog pass; stirrers). commands.js validates `r`.
- Deadline `until`: no try starts that would end past it (judged by the last try's time); a big pool's search stops at 70% of the time left (cutting a 30-dice recording to size takes 0.3-1 s); the recording loosens by about the square of its overshoot (fewer passes). The game: wait = 2.5 s + 70 ms a die past 8 (cap 4.5 s), the throw gets that deadline less 300 ms, MAX_DICE 24 -> 40 (the command's limit).
