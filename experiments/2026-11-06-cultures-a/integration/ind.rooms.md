## India (culture pack, six room types, built 2026-11-06)

Build → Cultures → India. The Jaipur-Amber region of Rajasthan in the 18th century: a Hindu merchant's haveli and its household kitchen, a Rajput palace hall, a Shiva temple's pillared hall (mandapa), a grain-and-spice merchant's bazaar shop, a stepwell (baori). Walls: `ind-lime` (ivory lime plaster with painted borders, an arched niche, the projecting jharokha window) for the shop and the kitchen; `ind-sandstone` (red sandstone, cusped blind arches, a jali window, its own square pillars at the corners) for the palace hall; `ind-temple` (golden sandstone carved in horizontal bands; no windows) for the temple hall. Doors: carved teak with brass studs. Roofs: flat terraces (`plaster` on lime, `flagstone` on sandstone). Floors: red sandstone, golden temple stone, a polished lime floor; a white mandana floor painting and a striped durrie. Every mesh is tier `ind`.
