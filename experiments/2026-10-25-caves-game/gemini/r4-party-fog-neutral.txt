#### 1. `player-desk.png`

1. **Description (2 sentences):**  
   The desktop interface displays a top-down isometric 3D tabletop encounter set against a gray checkerboard void representing fog of war. A large irregular sandy cavern containing five player pawns sits on the left, while a separate, fully enclosed stone dungeon room containing one pawn floats detached on the right.

2. **Is there a natural cave, and does it look like one?**  
   There is a cavern-like dirt area on the left with natural rock outcroppings and boulders, but it **does not** look like a convincing natural cave. Instead of enclosed cave walls and ambient darkness/fog, it is a flat sandy polygon floating in an infinite 3D engine checkerboard grid, several central rock pillars have glitchy checkerboard missing-texture mappings, and the perimeter abruptly drops off into the void.

3. **Broken or wrong elements (with location):**
   * **Missing/broken textures on pillars (Cavern center, left, and right):** Five stone spires/pillars (between Bram & Fen, right of Fen, near Dov, center, and left of Cora) are rendered with a gray-and-dark checkerboard placeholder material rather than rock textures.
   * **Fog of war rendered as raw checkerboard (Global background):** The unrevealed area is drawn as a transparent/UV test checkerboard grid instead of an atmospheric fog mask or solid blackness.
   * **Fog boundary artifacts / phantom rock geometry (Lower and upper perimeter of sandy area):** Faint gray checkerboard rock silhouettes poke into the fog below Dov, along the bottom edge, and around the edges of the clearing.
   * **Token nameplates obscuring tokens (All pawns):** The large black label pills (`Bram`, `Fen`, `Eli`, `Dov`, `Cora`, `Ann`) sit directly on top of the character models, obscuring nearly the entire pawn figure except for the yellow base.
   * **Detached room discontinuity (Far right):** Ann's room is completely isolated across the checkerboard void with no connecting hallway, tunnel, or wall occlusion.

#### 2. `player-phone.png`

2. **Is there a natural cave, and does it look like one?**  
   It contains the same sandy floor and rock clusters, but it **does not** look like a natural cave. The tall pillars still display the broken checkerboard missing-texture glitch, and the tight viewport framing makes it appear as an untextured 3D terrain asset floating in an exposed checkerboard void.
