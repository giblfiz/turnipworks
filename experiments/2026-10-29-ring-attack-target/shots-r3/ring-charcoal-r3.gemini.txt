### Buttons: Depiction & Action ([ring-charcoal.png](file://experiments/2026-10-29-ring-attack-target/shots-r3/ring-charcoal.png))

#### Left Screenshot (`Thorin` — player's pawn)
1. **`Turn`**: Depicts a wooden crate with a curved cream arrow wrapping around its base from left to right. **Action:** Rotate/change the pawn's facing direction.
2. **`Sheet`**: Depicts a parchment page with a teal pawn silhouette and two horizontal lines of text beneath it. **Action:** Open the character/stat sheet.
3. **`Damage`**: Depicts a red heart split down the center by a jagged crack. **Action:** Apply damage or adjust HP/health.
4. **`Attack`**: Depicts two crossed broadswords with silver blades, gold/bronze guards and pommels, and slate/teal grips. **Action:** Perform an attack with this pawn.
5. **`Eyes`**: Depicts a teal pawn with a large eyeball set into its spherical head. **Action:** Look through this pawn's eyes (first-person/pawn camera view or vision check).
6. **`All options`**: Depicts three stacked horizontal cream bars (hamburger menu). **Action:** Open the full context menu of actions.

#### Right Screenshot (`Goblin 1` — another pawn)
1. **`Sheet`**: Parchment sheet with a teal pawn silhouette and two lines below it. **Action:** View this pawn's stat sheet.
2. **`Damage`**: Cracked red heart. **Action:** Apply damage or modify this pawn's HP.
3. **`Target`**: A teal pawn surrounded by a red circular reticle with four inward-pointing arrowheads. **Action:** Mark/designate this pawn as the target being aimed at.
4. **`All options`**: Three stacked horizontal cream bars. **Action:** Open all available menu options for this pawn.

### Evaluation

**(1) Icon Family Cohesion**
* **Yes, they read as one cohesive painted icon family.** All buttons share the same diameter, dark charcoal/slate ground, beveled metallic rim, chunky painterly rendering style, and unified palette (teal pawns, crimson reds, warm parchment/cream, and muted bronze/wood).
* **Minor nuances:** The three newer icons (`Attack`, `Eyes`, `Target`) are slightly crisper and have a slightly brighter inner rim highlight than the older four (`Turn`, `Sheet`, `Damage`, `All options`), which look marginally softer/more anti-aliased. Thematically, `Turn` is the only spatial pawn action that uses a wooden crate rather than the teal pawn motif used in `Sheet`, `Eyes`, and `Target`, though its painting style still matches the set.

**(2) Legibility at This Size**
* **Yes.** Every button has a bold silhouette, strong value contrast against the dark charcoal ground, and low internal clutter. All seven icons are immediately readable at gameplay size.

**(3) New Buttons Assessment**
* **Attack (crossed swords): FITS** — Matches the charcoal ground, beveled rim, chunky painted brushwork, and warm metallic/teal palette; crossed swords read instantaneously as "Attack."
* **Target (red reticle around a pawn): FITS** — Combines the recurring teal pawn motif with the crimson palette from `Damage` on the same charcoal disc; the red crosshair ring with inward arrows around the pawn unmistakably communicates aiming at/marking that pawn.
* **Eyes (pawn with an eye): FITS** — Uses the same painted teal pawn and charcoal disc, and placing a high-contrast eye directly in the pawn's head clearly conveys looking from that pawn's perspective at this scale.
