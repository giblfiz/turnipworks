### Buttons: Icon Depiction & Action

**Left Screenshot (`Thorin` — player's pawn):**
1. **`Turn`:** A wooden crate encircled at the base by a curved cream arrow pointing right. **Action:** Rotate/change the pawn's facing direction.
2. **`Sheet`:** A curled parchment page showing a teal pawn silhouette above two horizontal lines. **Action:** Open the pawn's character/stat sheet.
3. **`Damage`:** A glossy red heart split down the center by a jagged crack. **Action:** Apply damage or modify HP.
4. **`Attack`:** Two crossed silver broadswords with dark crossguards and warm tan/gold grips. **Action:** Make an attack with this pawn.
5. **`All options`:** Three thick horizontal cream bars (hamburger menu). **Action:** Open the full overflow menu of actions.

**Right Screenshot (`Goblin 1` — other pawn):**
1. **`Sheet`:** Parchment page with a teal pawn silhouette and two lines of text. **Action:** View this pawn's stat sheet.
2. **`Damage`:** Cracked red heart split down the middle. **Action:** Apply damage or modify this pawn's HP.
3. **`Target`:** A teal 3D pawn surrounded by a red circular reticle with four corner brackets and four inward-pointing arrowheads. **Action:** Mark/designate this pawn as the target being aimed at.
4. **`All options`:** Three thick horizontal cream bars. **Action:** Open the full overflow menu of actions for this pawn.

### Evaluation

**(1) Do all the buttons look like one painted icon family?**
* **Yes.** All buttons share the same circular size, dark charcoal ground, beveled slate rim, soft directional lighting, and chunky hand-painted rendering style.
* **Minor outliers:**
  * **`Target`** is slightly more geometrically complex and crowded near the rim (combining a ring, four arrowheads, and four corner brackets) in a brighter red than `Damage`, whereas the rest of the family uses simpler, chunkier silhouettes with more negative space.
  * **`Turn`** uses a detailed 3D wooden crate rather than a bold graphic symbol, though its palette and brushwork still blend into the set.

**(2) Is each button legible at this size?**
* **Yes.** Every button reads clearly at gameplay scale. `Sheet`, `Damage`, `Attack`, and `All options` read at a glance; `Turn` reads clearly via the curved arrow; `Target` reads clearly as aiming at a pawn despite the denser reticle detail.

**(3) New Buttons (`Attack` and `Target`):**
* **Attack (crossed swords): FITS.** It matches the charcoal ground, rim, chunky proportions, lighting, and warm metal/cream palette of the existing icons, and reads immediately as "Attack."
* **Target (red reticle around a pawn): FITS.** It shares the exact ground, rim, and painted style, reuses the teal pawn motif from `Sheet` for visual continuity, and clearly communicates targeting a pawn. *(Optional refinement: if you ever simplify it, dropping the four outer corner brackets and keeping just the red ring + inward arrowheads would give it the same chunky breathing room as `Attack` and `Damage`.)*
