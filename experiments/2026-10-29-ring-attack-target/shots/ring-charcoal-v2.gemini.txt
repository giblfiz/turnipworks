### Buttons: Icon Depiction & Action

**Left Screenshot (`Thorin` — player's own pawn):**
1. **`Turn`:** A wooden crate with a curved cream arrow wrapping around its base → Rotate/change the facing direction of the pawn.
2. **`Sheet`:** A notched parchment page with a teal pawn silhouette and two horizontal lines → Open the pawn's character sheet / stat block.
3. **`Damage`:** A red heart split by a jagged crack down the center → Apply damage or modify the pawn's HP.
4. **`Attack`:** Two crossed steel broadswords with dark grips and brass pommels → Perform an attack with this pawn.
5. **`All options`:** Three horizontal cream-colored bars (hamburger menu) → Open the full submenu of actions/options for this pawn.

**Right Screenshot (`Goblin 1` — another pawn):**
1. **`Sheet`:** Same parchment page with a teal pawn silhouette and two lines → View this pawn's character sheet / stats.
2. **`Damage`:** Same cracked red heart → Apply damage or modify this pawn's HP.
3. **`Target`:** A 3D teal chess-style pawn centered inside a red circular crosshair/reticle with four inward-pointing ticks → Mark/designate this pawn as the target being aimed at.
4. **`All options`:** Same three horizontal cream-colored bars → Open the full submenu of options for this pawn.

### Evaluation

**(1) Do all the buttons look like one painted icon family?**
**Yes.** They share the same diameter, dark charcoal ground, beveled slate-grey circular rim, chunky stylized hand-painted shading, heavy drop shadows, and a unified color palette (`Target` directly echoes the teal pawn from `Sheet` and the crimson red from `Damage`; `Attack` echoes the warm off-white and brass/wood tones of `Sheet` and `Turn`).
* *Minor edge difference:* The four original icons (`Turn`, `Sheet`, `Damage`, `All options`) have a faint light outer edge highlight around the circular frame, whereas `Attack` and `Target` have a darker, cleaner outer edge, and `Target`'s red reticle sits slightly tighter against the rim bevel. Neither breaks family cohesion at gameplay size.

**(2) Is each button legible at this size?**
**Yes.** Every icon has bold, simple geometry and strong value contrast against the charcoal ground, making all six distinct icons immediately readable at screen size.

**(3) New Buttons Assessment**
* **Attack (crossed swords): FITS.** The charcoal ground, beveled slate rim, chunky blade proportions, warm steel/brass palette, and soft painted lighting match the existing set seamlessly, and crossed swords read unmistakably as "Attack" at a glance.
* **Target (red reticle around a pawn): FITS.** Combining the teal pawn silhouette with the red four-point crosshair clearly communicates "aim at / mark this pawn" while tying directly into the palette and painting style of `Sheet` and `Damage`. *(Minor optional tweak if ever touching it up: scale the red reticle ring down ~5–8% so it has a bit more breathing room inside the grey rim bevel.)*
