### Text Labels & Depictions

* **`R1`**: Teal pawn beside two horizontal text bars on a dog-eared parchment sheet (character sheet / inspect).
* **`R2`**: Cracked red heart with a dark minus bar to the right (damage / reduce health).
* **`R3`**: One hand passing a teal pawn to another hand beneath a curved orange arrow (give / transfer control).
* **`R4`**: Dark iron padlock with a brass keyhole plate (lock piece).

**Bottom Row (Target Candidates: `A` – `D`)**
* **`A`**: Large teal pawn inside a crimson ring with four inward-pointing cardinal triangles (top/bottom points touching the pawn).
* **`B`**: Slightly smaller teal pawn inside the same crimson ring with four inward-pointing cardinal triangles, leaving a clean gap of parchment around the pawn.
* **`C`**: Medium teal pawn inside a crimson ring with four slightly smaller inward-pointing triangles and softer shading.
* **`D`**: Taller, double-collared teal pawn inside a crimson ring with four inward-pointing triangles touching its head and base.

### Evaluation

**1. Style & Set Consistency (`A` – `D` vs. `R1` – `R4`)**
* **`A`**: **Yes.** Matches disc diameter, distressed gold rim, warm parchment ground, drop shadows, the glossy teal pawn from `R1`/`R3`, and the crimson red palette from `R2`.
* **`B`**: **Yes.** Same artist, palette, rim, and lighting; the slightly more compact pawn scale matches the negative space of `R1`–`R4` best.
* **`C`**: **Yes.** Same family and palette, with slightly flatter rendering on the red ring.
* **`D`**: **Yes.** Same family and palette, though the extra collar ridges on the pawn make it slightly busier than `R1`/`R3`.

**2. Readability at 31 px ("Target / Aim at Piece")**
* **`A` & `D`**: **Borderline.** The crimson ring and inward points signal hostility/targeting, but because the top and bottom triangles touch the pawn's head and base, they blur into the dark teal silhouette at 31 px.
* **`B` & `C`**: **Yes.** The crimson color and four sharp inward-pointing spikes read as an aggressive targeting reticle aimed at the pawn rather than a neutral camera-focus or selection ring. Having a sliver of light parchment between the red tips and the teal pawn keeps the inward arrows legible at 31 px.

**3. Single Best Candidate & Regeneration Verdict**
* **Best pick: `B`**. It has the sharpest inward reticle points and the cleanest separation between the red arrowheads and the teal pawn at 31 px.
* **Needs regeneration? No.** `B` works as-is and fits the family cleanly. *(Minor note: because the red reticle ring is concentric with the round button rim rather than crossing over the pawn's body, it looks slightly like an inner border at 31 px—but the four crimson inward spikes are distinct enough that a regeneration isn't necessary.)*
