### Evaluation

1. **Set & Artist Consistency (`R1`–`R4` vs. `A`–`D`):**
   * **A:** **Yes.** Matches the aged parchment ground, thin tarnished brass rim, disc size, dark outlines, and painted bevel style; the pawn's teal is slightly darker/more slate-toned than `R1`/`R3`.
   * **B:** **Yes (closest match).** Matches `R1`–`R4` in disc size, brass rim, parchment vignette, brushwork, and palette (`R2` crimson red and `R1`/`R3` teal pawn with crisp specular highlights).
   * **C:** **Mostly, with a size discrepancy.** Painting style and palette match, but the disc is slightly smaller (about 1–2 px narrower radius) than `R1`–`R4` and the other candidates.
   * **D:** **Yes.** Matches disc size, rim, parchment ground, and palette well, with slightly softer shading on the pawn and a slightly brighter red ring.

2. **Legibility at 31 px:**
   * **All four (`A`–`D`):** Read clearly as "target this pawn" at 31 px. Combining the circle, four triangles, and four corner brackets makes the red graphic a bit dense at 31 px, but **B** and **D** keep the clearest parchment gap around the pawn's head and torso so the central pawn doesn't merge into the reticle. **A** and **C** pinch the space above the pawn's head.

3. **Best Candidate & Regeneration Check:**
   * **Best pick: `B`.** It has the cleanest negative space around the pawn, the sharpest brushwork, accurate disc size, and the best teal/crimson palette match to `R1`–`R3`.
   * **Needs regeneration?** **No.** `B` is usable as-is (the bottom triangle overlapping the pawn base and the extra corner brackets make it slightly busy at 31 px, but not enough to require regeneration).
