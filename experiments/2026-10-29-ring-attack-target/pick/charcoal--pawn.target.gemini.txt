- **`B`**: A tall, smooth teal pawn in the same style of red ring-and-bracket targeting reticle, with a brighter top-left rim highlight.

### 1. Fit with `R1`–`R4` (Ground, Rim, Style, Palette, Disc Size)

Across all four candidates, the dark charcoal ground and teal pawn material match `R1`/`R3`, but the **reticle design and red palette drift slightly from the set**: the bright vermilion/orange-red is more saturated and flat than `R2`’s deep painterly crimson, and the combination of ring + 4 triangles + 4 corner L-brackets feels more like a modern tactical HUD than the physical fantasy objects in `R1`–`R4`.

- **`A`**: **Good match.** Disc size, charcoal ground, and rim match `R1`–`R4`. The pawn has the closest dark-teal value to `R1`, though the bottom red triangle overlaps the front of the pawn’s base.
- **`B`**: **Fair match.** Disc size and ground match, but the upper-left outer rim has a noticeably harsher white highlight than `R1`–`R4`, and the pawn leans slightly brighter cyan.
- **`C`**: **Weakest match.** The disc diameter is noticeably smaller than `R1`–`R4` (at both 62 px and 31 px), and the heavier red strokes crowd the interior.
- **`D`**: **Best technical match.** Disc size, charcoal ground, and even slate rim match `R1`–`R4` cleanly, and the pawn sits inside the ring without awkward overlaps.

---

### 2. Readability at 31 px

- **All four (`A`–`D`)** read immediately as *"target this pawn"* at 31 px—the high contrast between the red reticle, dark ground, and central teal pawn carries the action clearly.
- **`D`** and **`A`** read cleanest because they preserve a dark gap around the pawn silhouette.
- **`B`** gets slightly pinched where the top triangle meets the pawn’s head, and **`C`** feels cramped due to the smaller disc and thicker reticle lines.

---

### 3. Single Best Candidate & Regeneration Verdict

- **Best pick: `D`** (with **`A`** a close second). `D` has the most consistent disc size and rim lighting alongside `R1`–`R4`, and the cleanest separation between the pawn silhouette and the reticle triangles at both 62 px and 31 px.
- **Does it need regeneration?** `D` is usable now, but if you want a strict style match with `R1`–`R4`, **regenerate** with two fixes:
  1. **Drop the four outer corner L-brackets** (keep a simpler crosshair/ring so it reads less like a modern HUD and has more breathing room near the rim at 31 px).
  2. **Shift the red toward `R2`’s deeper, more painterly crimson** rather than bright scarlet.
