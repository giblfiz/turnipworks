- **Top row:** `R1`, `R2`, `R3`, `R4`
- **Bottom row:** `A`, `B`, `C`, `D`

### What Each Icon Depicts
- **R1:** Blue-teal pawn with two horizontal text bars on a dog-eared parchment sheet (inspect / character sheet).
- **R2:** Cracked red heart with a dark minus bar beside it (damage / lose HP).
- **R3:** Two hands passing a blue-teal pawn beneath a curved orange arrow (give / transfer).
- **R4:** Dark iron padlock with a brass keyhole plate (lock).
- **A:** Two crossed swords (pale silver blades, brown grips, small brass pommels).
- **B:** Two crossed swords (medium steel blades, brown grips, dark iron guards and pommels).
- **C:** Two crossed swords (darker-outlined steel blades, warm amber/brass grips and pommels, dark iron guards).
- **D:** Two crossed swords (light steel blades, brown grips, larger dark iron guards and pommels).

---

### 1. Set & Artist Consistency with R1–R4
All four candidates (**A–D**) match **R1–R4** in disc size, weathered brass rim, and warm inner-shadowed parchment ground:
- **A:** Weakest fit — the blades are too bright/white with faint outlines, lacking the bold dark contours and deep value contrast of R1–R4.
- **B:** Good fit — darker steel shading and dark iron hardware echo R4's ironwork, with warm brown grips.
- **C:** Strongest fit — crisp dark outer contours on the blades, clear bevel contrast, and warm brass/amber hilt tones that match the palette of R3/R4.
- **D:** Good fit — very close to B (dark iron pommels/guards, warm brown grips), with slightly paler upper blades than C.

### 2. Readability at 31 px
- **Yes:** All four read as crossed swords ("Attack") at 31 px, largely carried by the dark crossguards and angled hilts in the lower half. **C** reads clearest at 31 px because its darker blade outline keeps the upper tips from washing into the cream parchment (which happens noticeably on **A**).

### 3. Single Best Candidate & Regeneration Verdict
- **Best pick:** **C**.
- **Needs regeneration?** **Usable as-is, with one caveat:** unlike R1–R4 (which place dark teal, deep red, or dark gunmetal against the light parchment), all four candidates place **light silver steel directly on pale cream parchment**, lowering upper-half contrast at 31 px. If you regenerate, darkening the blades toward R4's gunmetal iron (or heavier dark outlines around the blades) would make the silhouette pop at 31 px at the same strength as R1–R4.
