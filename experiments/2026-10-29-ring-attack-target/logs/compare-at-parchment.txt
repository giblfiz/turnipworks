### Pair Comparisons

#### 1. Attack (`A` vs. `B`) — **Winner: `B`**
* **Set Fit (Ground, Rim, Disc Size, Brushwork, Palette, Lighting):** While both `A` and `B` share the set's disc size, antique gold rim, and warm parchment ground (so `A` is structurally compatible), **`B` belongs much better to the `R1`–`R4` set**. The existing artist consistently renders metal and neutral dark elements in a signature dark slate/forged-iron palette (`R4`'s padlock, `R2`'s minus bar, `R1`'s bars) with crisp top-left edge highlights and warm brass/amber accents. `B` uses that exact dark-iron blade treatment, brushwork, and lighting, whereas `A`'s bright silver-white blades introduce a pale metallic value that clashes with the light parchment ground.
* **Readability at 31 px:** **`B` reads much better at 31 px.** Because `B`'s blades are dark slate-iron against a light cream parchment ground, the crossed-swords silhouette has strong value contrast and reads instantly. In `A`, the pale silver blades have very low value contrast against the light parchment interior and wash out at 31 px.

#### 2. Target (`C` vs. `D`) — **Winner: `C`**
* **Set Fit (Ground, Rim, Disc Size, Brushwork, Palette, Lighting):** **`C` belongs clearly to the `R1`–`R4` set** (`D` does not fit as well).
  * **Disc Size & Rim:** `C` matches the exact outer disc diameter and gold rim proportions of `R1`–`R4`, whereas `D`'s disc is slightly smaller and heavier-rimmed.
  * **Brushwork, Proportions & Palette:** The central pawn in `C` has the exact slender silhouette, crisp contour brushwork, deep rich teal palette, and sharp top-left specular highlight of the pawn in `R1`. Its target ring uses the same deep crimson red as the heart in `R2`. By contrast, `D` has softer, chunkier brushwork, a squatter/wider pawn, and an overly thick, warmer-red ring with heavy drop shadows.
* **Readability at 31 px:** **`C` reads much better at 31 px.** Because `C` places a thinner crimson ring near the outer rim around a slender pawn, a clean halo of pale parchment negative space remains between the red arrowheads and the pawn, keeping both shapes distinct at 31 px. In `D`, the thicker ring is pulled inward and its bulky triangles crowd right against the squat pawn, making the center look cramped and muddled at 31 px.

ATTACK: B
TARGET: C
