### 3. Visual QA Questions

#### (1) Do ALL the buttons look like one painted icon family (ground, rim, size, brushwork, palette, lighting)? Name any that look like a different set, and why.
- **Overall**: Yes, all 28 buttons share the **same circular frame family** (identical diameter, dark slate-blue radial-gradient ground, and beveled dark gunmetal rim) and the same general **soft digital-painted volumetric rendering** with top-left lighting and dark drop shadows.
- **Minor stylistic outliers within the family**:
  - **`stunned`, `exhaustion +1`, and `exhaustion -1`**: Use bright yellow cartoon/emoji-style heads with dark comic linework for facial features, contrasting with the more painterly fantasy heads in `unconscious`, `paralyzed`, and `frightened` and the featureless mannequin head in `blinded`.
  - **`✓ prone`**: Drawn at a much smaller scale with flat, dark brown tones that leave most of the circular ground empty, unlike the bold, circle-filling, saturated subjects of the other icons.
  - **`invisible`**: Uses a flatter 2D glowing cyan stencil/cutout silhouette rather than 3D volumetric shading (appropriate to the subject, but visually flatter than the rest).
  - **`Indicators` (inner ring)**: The gold star fills almost the entire circle edge-to-edge with hard geometric bevels rather than the softer organic brushwork used on the other icons.

#### (2) Is each legible at this size?
- **Mostly yes, with a few weak spots**:
  - **`✓ prone`** has the poorest icon legibility at this size: the horizontal brown mannequin is very small, thin, and low-contrast against the dark slate ground, looking like a thin brown bar until inspected closely.
  - **`incapacitated`**: The pale hands read well, but the dark maroon `X` behind them has low contrast against the dark background.
  - **`exhaustion -1`**: The green `-` bar is relatively small compared to the face, requiring a closer look than the larger red `+` on `exhaustion +1`.
  - **Label occlusion on `Conditions` (inner ring)**: The floating `Conditions` tooltip covers the middle of the curved `Conditions` text label, and the `Indicators` button rim slightly clips the top of the `Brakka` nameplate.

#### (3) Could any two be confused with each other?
- **`exhaustion +1` and `exhaustion -1`** *(High risk)*: Both use the exact same yellow sweating face artwork; only the small red `+` vs. green `-` on the right differentiates them.
- **`stunned` vs. `exhaustion +1` / `exhaustion -1`** *(Moderate risk)*: All three feature a round yellow cartoon head at a similar scale; at a fast peripheral glance they look very similar until you spot the orbiting stars vs. `+`/`-`.
- **`Turn` and `Raise / lower` (inner ring)** *(Moderate risk)*: Both use the exact same brown wooden crate accompanied by a white arrow (curved horizontal arrow vs. vertical double-headed arrow).
- **`paralyzed`, `unconscious`, and `frightened`** *(Low-to-moderate risk)*: All three are blue/purple humanoid heads filling the circle, though their secondary cues (lightning bolts, white crescent moon, screaming hands-on-cheeks pose) separate them once focused.
- **`concentrating` and `incapacitated`** *(Low risk)*: Both feature a pair of pale hands in the lower half of the circle, though the bright glowing blue orb in `concentrating` sets them apart clearly.
