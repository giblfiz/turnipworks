### 2. What Each Button Depicts & Whether It Fits Its Label

#### Outer Arc — Conditions List (18 buttons)
1. **`blinded`** *(D&D condition)*: Depicts a dark grey featureless head with a white cloth blindfold tied across the eyes (knot tails on the right). **Fits** the condition directly.
2. **`charmed`** *(D&D condition)*: Depicts a large pink/magenta heart containing a spiral swirl, with two tiny pink hearts floating at the upper right. **Fits** the charmed/infatuated condition.
3. **`frightened`** *(D&D condition)*: Depicts a purple face in an Edvard Munch *The Scream*-style pose—wide white eyes, open screaming mouth, and both hands pressed to its cheeks. **Fits** fear/terror clearly.
4. **`grappled`** *(D&D condition)*: Depicts a reddish-brown hand clamping tightly onto a paler wrist/forearm with small black stress lines around the grip. **Fits** being physically grabbed/held.
5. **`invisible`** *(D&D condition)*: Depicts a full-body humanoid figure outlined in glowing cyan/blue with wispy vapor/smoke trails dissolving to the right and a translucent interior showing the parchment ground beneath. **Fits** invisibility/fading from sight.
6. **`poisoned`** *(D&D condition)*: Depicts a round green glass potion bottle stoppered with a cork and marked with a black skull-and-crossbones on its front. **Fits** poison clearly.
7. **`✓ prone`** *(D&D condition, currently active)*: Depicts a small brown humanoid figure lying flat face-down horizontally across the center of the button. **Fits** the meaning of lying prone, though the figure is drawn very small and muddy.
8. **`restrained`** *(D&D condition)*: Depicts a red humanoid torso and upper arms tightly bound in multiple horizontal coils of white/cream rope with a central vertical knot. **Fits** being tied up/restrained.
9. **`stunned`** *(D&D condition)*: Depicts a bright yellow dazed face with uneven/swirly eyes, an open grimace, sweat droplets, and a ring of four-pointed yellow stars orbiting above the head. **Fits** the classic "seeing stars" stunned trope.
10. **`unconscious`** *(D&D condition)*: Depicts a dark indigo/navy-blue head tilted to the left with closed eyes, resting beside a pale cream crescent moon. **Fits** sleep/unconsciousness.
11. **`paralyzed`** *(D&D condition)*: Depicts a medium-blue head and shoulders with wide staring eyes, clenched teeth, and jagged cyan lightning bolts crackling around the head and neck. **Fits** paralysis via rigid electric shock/seizure.
12. **`incapacitated`** *(D&D condition)*: Depicts two grey-gloved/bandaged hands held side-by-side over a red `X` behind them. **Fits** the D&D rule of being unable to take actions or reactions ("hands tied/crossed out").
13. **`dead`** *(D&D/character condition)*: Depicts a grey stone rounded-top headstone on a tiered base with a dark cross carved on the front. **Fits** death directly.
14. **`concentrating`** *(D&D condition)*: Depicts two dark cupped hands holding up a glowing blue/cyan orb surrounded by five smaller glowing motes. **Fits** maintaining focus on an active spell.
15. **`exhaustion +1`** *(exhaustion level up)*: Depicts a warm ochre-yellow weary bald head on the left (heavy drooping eyelids, sweat drop, open sighing mouth) paired with a bold red `+` sign on the right. **Fits** adding a level of exhaustion.
16. **`exhaustion -1`** *(exhaustion level down)*: Depicts the same ochre-yellow weary bald head on the left paired with a green horizontal `-` bar on the right. **Fits** removing a level of exhaustion.
17. **`✓ hexed`** *(custom condition, currently active)*: Depicts a tilted white/cream luggage or price tag on a string loop bearing a teal checkmark (`✓`) in the center. **Fits** as a generic "active custom condition tag" icon (corresponding to the custom `hexed` tag on `Brakka`), though it depicts a generic checked label tag rather than literal curse/hex imagery.
18. **`other...`** *(Other... / add custom condition)*: Depicts a golden feather quill writing on a small scroll/sheet of parchment accompanied by a teal `+` sign on the right. **Fits** writing/adding a custom condition.

### 3. Visual Audit Questions

#### (1) Do ALL the buttons look like one painted icon family (ground, rim, size, brushwork, palette, lighting)? Name any that look like a different set, and why.
- **Shared container/frame**: All 28 buttons share the exact same circular medallion base—identical diameter, warm parchment/cream radial ground, and double-beveled tan-gold/bronze rim with a soft drop shadow.
- **Where the icon artwork splits into different sets/styles**:
  1. **Inner-ring UI icons + `✓ hexed` and `other...` vs. the 14 D&D conditions**: The 10 inner-ring buttons plus `✓ hexed` and `other...` form a tight, unified **tabletop UI icon set** with a restrained, matte palette (slate-teal, warm ochre-gold, pewter/charcoal, and muted brick red), consistent dark-brown outlines, and soft top-left lighting with subtle drop shadows on the parchment ground. By contrast, the 14 D&D condition icons (`blinded` through `concentrating`) shift into bright, high-saturation comic/emoji colors (neon lemon yellow, bubblegum magenta, lime green, electric cyan, violet) and lack a unified character model across the heads (`blinded` is grey, `frightened` is purple, `stunned` is lemon yellow, `unconscious` is navy, `paralyzed` is electric blue, `exhaustion` is ochre-gold).
  2. **`stunned`**: Stands out even among the condition heads—it uses a flat, unshaded neon lemon-yellow fill and thin, crisp cartoon ink lines with flat white four-point stars, looking like a 2D vector/comic emoji compared to the warmer, softer painterly brushwork of `exhaustion +1` / `exhaustion -1`.
  3. **`✓ prone`** (and to a lesser degree **`invisible`**): Breaks the **scale and framing rules** of the family. Whereas nearly every other icon is a bold close-up head, hand, or single object filling ~60–75% of the circle, `✓ prone` is a tiny full-body figure drawn horizontally across the middle third, leaving large empty areas of parchment ground above and below.
  4. **`concentrating` and `paralyzed`**: Use bright airbrushed/glowing cyan lighting effects (the glowing gradient orb and electric lightning arcs) rather than the flat/matte painted surfaces of icons like `dead`, `restrained`, `grappled`, or `blinded`.
  5. **`charmed` and `poisoned`**: Look flatter and more graphic/vector-like (bright magenta spiral heart; neon green flask with a flat black stencil skull) than the more textured painterly icons (`restrained`, `dead`, `grappled`).

#### (2) Is each legible at this size?
- **Mostly yes**, with two notable exceptions:
  - **`✓ prone` is poorly legible at this size**: The tiny, narrow brown figure lying horizontally has very low internal contrast (dark brown lines on medium brown fill) and looks like a brown twig, log, or slug until you read the text label.
  - **`incapacitated` is slightly crowded at a glance**: The two grey hands with cuffs at the top layered over a red `X` require a moment to parse as hands rather than mittens, boots, or armor plates.
  - **`invisible`** is readable as a dissolving humanoid silhouette, though its pale cyan/white tones against the light cream ground have lower contrast than the rest of the arc.
  - *(UI text note)*: The curved `Conditions` label on the inner ring is partially covered by the dark rectangular hover tooltip `Conditions`, and the `Brakka` nameplate is slightly clipped at the top by the `Indicators` button.

#### (3) Could any two be confused with each other?
- **`exhaustion +1` and `exhaustion -1`** *(high confusion risk)*: They use the **exact same weary yellow head artwork** on the left and sit right next to each other on the ring; only the small `+` (red) vs. `-` (green) symbol on the right distinguishes the icons (which is especially easy to confuse at a glance or for red-green colorblind users).
- **`stunned` vs. `exhaustion +1` / `exhaustion -1`**: Both feature a yellow bald head with distressed eyes and sweat droplets; in peripheral vision both read as "yellow distressed face."
- **`paralyzed` vs. `frightened` (and `unconscious`)**: Both `paralyzed` and `frightened` are front-facing cool-toned (blue/purple) grimacing/screaming heads (`unconscious` is also a blue head adjacent to `paralyzed`, though its closed eyes and moon help separate it).
- **`incapacitated`, `grappled`, and `restrained`**: All three visually depict physical hand/body restriction (`incapacitated` = two hands over red X; `grappled` = hand grabbing wrist; `restrained` = roped torso), so players unfamiliar with the icons may mix up which means "cannot take actions" vs. "speed 0 / held."
- **`Turn` vs. `Raise / lower` (on the inner ring)**: Both use the exact same 3D brown wooden crate illustration, differing only in whether the arrow wraps around the base (`Turn`) or points vertically (`Raise / lower`).
