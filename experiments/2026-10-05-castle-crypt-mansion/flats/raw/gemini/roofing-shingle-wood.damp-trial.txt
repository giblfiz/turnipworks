Here is the analysis of the two roof textures.

### Version A
*   **Obviousness Score:** 5 (Obvious)
*   **Landmarks:** The repeating grid is immediately visible due to a recurring cluster of three highly distinct shingles:
    1.  A very bright, almost yellow shingle.
    2.  A very dark shingle with a prominent dark knot.
    3.  A pale, greyish shingle near the top right of the tile.

### Version B
*   **Obviousness Score:** 1 (Very subtle)
*   **Landmarks:** The prominent landmarks from Version A have been specifically toned down, making them blend in with the surrounding shingles. The repetition is now very difficult to detect without close inspection.

### Comparison
Version **B** shows the repeating grid far less. It has successfully removed the obvious landmarks without losing its natural piece-to-piece colour variation; the texture does not look flat, washed out, or smeared and is a much better seamless tile.
