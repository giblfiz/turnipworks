Here is the analysis of the two roof textures.

### Version A

*   **Repetition Score:** 5 (Obvious)
*   **Landmarks:** The grid is immediately obvious due to two highly distinct, adjacent tiles that repeat nine times: a very dark, almost purplish-brown tile, and a much brighter, yellowish-orange tile just below and to its right.

### Version B

*   **Repetition Score:** 1 (Very subtle)
*   **Landmarks:** The prominent landmark tiles from version A have been skillfully removed. You have to search carefully to find any repeating patterns.

### Comparison

Version **B** shows the repeating grid far less. It has successfully eliminated the obvious landmarks without losing its natural piece-to-piece colour variation; it does **not** look flat, washed out, or smeared. The detail and subtle tonal shifts are well-preserved.
