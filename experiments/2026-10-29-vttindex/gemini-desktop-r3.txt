### 2. Review & Verdict

#### `desktop-top.png`
**VERDICT: FIX**

**Problems:**
1. **Table is barely visible without scrolling:** The large header, 3 long paragraphs of intro copy, and filter panel push the table below the fold—only the header and 1.5 rows appear on a 1366×900 display.
2. **Horizontal clipping:** The rightmost column (`P...`) is cut off at the right viewport edge without an explicit horizontal scrollbar or indicator.
3. **Stray visual artifact:** Stray tick marks (`^^` / quotes) float immediately above the `Product↑` header.
4. **Legend clarity:** The chips are understandable in meaning (green/yellow/red/gray), but lack a clear section label or container, floating loosely between filters and the table.

---

#### `desktop-detail.png`
**VERDICT: FIX**

**Problems:**
1. **Not clear the panel continues below:** There is no visible scrollbar, fade mask, or overflow hint. The panel has a rounded bottom border cleanly enclosing `Offline / self-host`, falsely signaling that the content ends there.
2. **`[source]` links have poor legibility:** The light mustard/bronze color on white fails standard contrast expectations and lacks underlines, making them look like inert low-contrast annotations rather than clickable links.
3. **Insufficient backdrop dimming:** The overlay behind the modal is very faint, leaving background text (`How to read it`, table headers) visually competing with the modal content.
4. **Table horizontal clipping persists:** The background table's right edge (`P`) remains clipped off-screen.
