# Turnip Works style bakeoff

The active house-style candidates are:

- **A — graphic cel:** `graphic-cel-tavern-v1.png`. Strong outlines, hard graphic shadow shapes, simplified materials, and the most distinctive visual identity.
- **B — chunky hand-painted:** `chunky-handpainted-tavern-v1.png`. Broad bevels, soft painted gradients, restrained texture, and a neutral stylized-game finish likely to accommodate imported miniatures.
- **C — heroic stylized PBR:** `heroic-stylized-pbr-tavern-v1.png` (**not distributed**: removed 2026-10-21 because it was conditioned on a third-party screenshot; its prompt stays as the record). Thick miniature-safe forms, smooth simplified surfaces, saturated component colors, broad specular highlights, and strong ambient occlusion. It used Harry's 2026-09-30 Hero Forge screenshot solely as a style reference; the supplied character and equipment were excluded.
- **D — heroic hand-painted (selected target):** `heroic-handpainted-tavern-v1.png`. C's stout miniature-compatible geometry and component separation with B's matte painted gradients, restrained brush texture, and softer material response. `heroic-handpainted-alchemists-lab-v1.png` tests a dense specialty room; `heroic-handpainted-multilevel-dungeon-v1.png` tests sparse connected spaces, elevation changes, water, and a slightly dimmer lighting baseline.

Harry selected D as the Turnip Works house-style target after reviewing the A/B/C bakeoff and the C-geometry/B-finish hybrid. The D tavern, alchemist's lab, and multilevel dungeon are the accepted initial style sample set: dense mundane, dense magical, and sparse architectural scenes. A, B, and C remain useful comparison plates rather than rejected or production asset lineages. Exact prompts and provenance notes sit beside each image.
