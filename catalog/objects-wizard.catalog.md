# Wizard's tower gaps — the wizard / alchemist tower type (2026-10-11)

Objects the wizard's-tower rooms (`catalog/rooms-wizard.md`, batch `experiments/2026-10-11-wizard-tower/wizard/`) want and the catalog lacked. Harry, 2026-10-11: "We probably need a alchimist / wizards tower tile set." Most of what a tower wants already had a line in `objects-alchemist.catalog.md` and `fantasy-core.catalog.md` (athanor, still, reagent shelves, lectern, scroll rack, pylon, portal arch, scrying pool, orrery); these are the floor-standing pieces that were only catalogued as table-top smalls, the wall kit's two special pieces, and the menagerie.

The tower's wall style is a new kit family, `tower-stone` (warm buff ashlar with a plum-grey band course): `wall-straight--tower-stone` (two versions), `wall-doorway--tower-stone`, `wall-window-small--tower-stone` (a tall lancet of leaded glass). The apothecary shop uses the existing `half-timber` family with one new piece, `wall-shop-window--half-timber`. Arcane iconography is kept OFF walls and furniture on purpose (a "flavors" system is coming): circles and star charts are separate flat props.
