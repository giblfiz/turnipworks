# China rooms: the CHN culture pack (2026-11-06, cultures-a)

The China type in the Build tray (Regional → China): six rooms of a well-off official's courtyard house in mid-Qing Beijing and a Buddhist temple hall of that city. Each room is one line of the culture skeleton (`tools/spawn-tuner/js/solver2.js`, the `ca:` and `chn:` blocks; `experiments/2026-11-06-cultures-a/integration/`). The market street and the courtyard are outdoor rooms. The Buddha and the temple's furniture are placed only by the temple hall.
