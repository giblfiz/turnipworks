# Room recipes — monster lair (2026-10-11)

The lair type's own rooms (`tools/spawn-tuner/ROOMS.md` → Monster lair). Same format and validation as
`room-recipes.md`. A lair room is defined by its occupant: the four dens (dragon, spider, goblin, beast) share one
skeleton (bed, hoard, feeding spot, warning signs, bolt hole) and differ in dressing. The dragon's and the spider's
recipes are older and live in `room-recipes.md` / `rooms-loot.md` / `rooms-environment.md`.
